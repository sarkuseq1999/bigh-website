**Current working mission:** “Our mission is to support cellular health and mental energy, helping people live life to the fullest.” Mo proposed this wording with “their life”; the recommended final polish removes “their.” Saved as our working version, still editable.

**Wording and evidence:** Prefer “support” over “build healthier cells” for more measured wording; this does not remove the need for evidence. Each product needs its own evidence-based explanation, including evidence for any claimed link between cellular effects and mental benefits.
