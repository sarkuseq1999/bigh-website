## About BiGH

BiGH is the acronym for Be in Good Health. We are a natural product company that focus on the art of incorporating natural ingredients with modern science to help our customers live a healthier and more active lifestyle.

We do it because we believe nature nor science alone is enough to provide our body with the optimum amount of nourishment. However, when Science is combined with Nature or Nature is guided by Science, the result is much improved bioavailability (absorption), synergy and overall effectiveness.

Whether we’re making organic plant-based wholefood supplements, natural antioxidant supplements or doctor formulated supplements, we will always start by selecting the highest quality ingredients which are sourced from areas or regions that are best suited for its natural growth. Then, with the help of our scientific teams, we want to make sure that these key ingredients will synergistically enrich our formulation. And finally through cGMP certified manufactures and numerous lab tests, we will ensure our customers with natural supplements that are safe, consistent and incredibly nutritious.

## Three Guiding Principles

**The Greatest Wealth is Health**

We just can’t agree more with this quote from Virgil. After all, who’s going to take care of our loved ones if we don’t have good health? How can we have the freedom to do things we like if our health suffers? What’s the purpose of all those tangible and intangible things in this world if we don’t have the health to enjoy it? 

**Helping Other Enriches Our Own Life**

Here at BiGH, we believe the best form of happiness is obtained through helping others. Family, friend, society, anything counts as long as we are genuinely giving our heart to help those who needs it. The more we helped, the happier and more at-peace our state of mind will be. That’s what motivates us and also the reason why we want to make the best natural supplements for customers all over the world. 

**Passion for Lifelong Learning**

Life is an adventure, we want to live to its fullest, and we want to experience every detail that it has to offer. There might be endless paths to take or choices to make, but we believe the best way to guide us to the right direction is through lifelong learning and self improvement, to become the better version of ourselves. We want to improve our crafts, we want to improve our character and we want to improve our health, and so much more.
