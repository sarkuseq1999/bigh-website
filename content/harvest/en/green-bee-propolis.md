## Brazilian Green Propolis

The Brazilian Green Propolis is widely known for its therapeutical properties backed by hundreds of scientific studies that leads to its richness in flavonoid content and antioxidant property. ## Green Propolis from Minas Gerais

BiGH uses propolis exclusively from Minas Gerais, Brazil, because in Minas Gerais there are large areas of untouched baccaris dracumculifolia and eucalyptus forest not polluted with pesticides so high quality green propolis that are rich in flavonoids and antioxidants can be produced with assistance from Africanized honey bee.\*
