## Certified Organic

Organic means produce that is grown and processed without the use of synthetic chemical-based fertilizers, pesticides and genetically modified organisms (GMOs). The Certified Organic seal (USDA Organic) ensures that the entire product contains 95 percent or more certified organic content, which is a very high standard enforced from start to finish – every step along the way.

## Our Commitment

At BiGH, we really care about what goes into our body. We believe that human body is best designed to digest and absorb pure ingredients. Ingredients that are organically grown and in area or region that’s best suited for its natural growth. That’s why we’re committed to use certified organic ingredients in our formulation and also the reason that many of our products carry the USDA Organic seal.
