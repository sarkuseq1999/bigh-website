Final generation prompt
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original still artwork for an immersive, animated BiGH cellular-health website hero; create ONE finished image, not a website mockup or a collage.
Primary request: a visually exquisite, sculptural interpretation of a living cell. One large, softly asymmetrical translucent form floats weightlessly in a spacious, serene environment. It should feel alive, tactile, elegant and memorable, with the craft of a gallery glass sculpture and the intimacy of cinematic macro photography.
Subject: a delicate translucent outer membrane with an organic, gently folded silhouette, not a perfect ball. Within it, a softly luminous pearlescent central structure and a few graceful, folded inner forms create beautiful depth. Fine striations, subtle refraction, thin iridescent edges and soft internal light reward looking closely. The anatomy is artistic and cell-inspired, not an educational diagram or an actual microscope photograph.
Composition: wide landscape 16:9 hero artwork at high resolution. The single complete cell sculpture occupies the right-hand portion prominently, with its center around 68% of the image width. Leave the left 40% quiet and spacious for the website headline “Good health starts with your cells.” to be added later; DO NOT render any words. The entire sculpture must fit inside the frame with generous space around it. Keep the backdrop smooth, restrained and luminous, with only a faint soft reflection or shadow beneath the floating form. It should feel like one coherent scene with ample breathing room.
Materials and lighting: exceptionally convincing translucent material, natural pearlescence, soft internal glow, controlled cinematic light, delicate warm and cool interplay in the refractions. Elegant, restrained color; luminous highlights without blown-out whites. Rich three-dimensional depth without visual clutter.
Animation readiness: crisp still frame, a distinct continuous outer silhouette, clear separation from the calm background, internal details that could support a very gentle rotation and a slow movement of light later.
Constraints: no text, no lettering, no logos, no product bottles, no people, no labels, no interface, no extra floating cells, no decorative particles. Avoid spiky virus shapes, medical horror, a toy-like jelly ball, busy textbook organelles, generic neon science-fiction graphics, star fields, oversaturated rainbow colors, or dramatic motion blur. Make the object itself the beautiful and arresting focal point.
