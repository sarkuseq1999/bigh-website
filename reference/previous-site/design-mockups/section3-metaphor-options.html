Brainstorm · Section 3 — The Science

How should the science look?

All three tell the same story. What changes is the central visual and how it moves as you scroll.

1 · Fog isn't aging→ 2 · Inside one brain cell→ 3 · 1,000+ power plants→ 4 · Fuel drops · waste builds→ 5 · A brain needs fuel + protection
Option A

The Descent

The camera flies inward — your head → one brain cell → the glowing power plant inside. Each scroll beat drops a level deeper.

↓  Zoom is the scroll mechanic; depth = beat ↓  The mitochondrion is the destination
Most cinematic. Biggest "wow." Needs the most art / asset work to feel smooth.
BRIGHT → DIM → REVIVED
Option B My lean

The Living Power Plant

The mitochondrion (your existing glowing asset) stays centered and changes state as you scroll: bright → fuel drains → waste chokes it → fuel + protection revive it.

◑  One object, changing state = beat ◑  Reuses the asset you already have
Most emotional + buildable. You watch it struggle, then revive. Less fly-through spectacle than A.
↑ fuel ↓ waste
Option C

The Energy Gauge

An explainer diagram. A central energy meter with two visible forces — fuel charging it up, waste draining it. Each beat reveals another part of the diagram.

▤  Infographic reveal = beat ▤  Clearest "teaching" of the mechanism
Clearest, most literal. Easy to make accurate — but risks feeling clinical on this poetic brand.

My lean: B, opening with a short taste of A's zoom as the intro — reuses your asset, lands emotionally, still teaches.
