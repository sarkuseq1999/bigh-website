01 The Science

The mitochondrial theory of brain aging.

Mitochondria are the power plants inside every cell — they make the energy, ATP, that thinking, remembering, and focusing all depend on. With age they decay: oxidative damage builds up in their lipids, proteins, RNA and DNA. They make less energy and leak more damage. That decline tracks with the memory loss and mental fatigue of aging.

NuriCell is built on a formula known in the research literature as CellGen, developed by Dr. Jian Kang Liu, a biochemist whose landmark work was done in the Division of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology at the University of California, Berkeley, alongside the renowned scientist Dr. Bruce Ames.

Finding Liu J. et al. · PNAS · Vol. 99 · 2002

Two mitochondrial nutrients partially reversed age-associated brain mitochondrial decay in old rats.

Dr. Liu's team fed aged rats two mitochondrial nutrients — acetyl-L-carnitine and R-alpha-lipoic acid — and measured the result. The animals improved their performance on memory tasks. The combination worked better than either nutrient alone, and electron-microscope imaging showed aged mitochondria structurally restored toward a younger state.
