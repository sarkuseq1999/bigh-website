Seven short horizontal brush strokes of black sumi ink on plain white paper, stacked one above another with wide white space between them. Each is a single confident stroke from a soft round calligraphy brush, each a slightly different length and weight: the brush lands loaded and pressed at the left, travels right with a slight natural waver, and lifts into a tapering dry-brush flick at the right end where the bristles split into fine flying-white streaks. Ragged paper-tooth edges, a faint bleed into the fibres. Pure white background, no text, no seal, no signature, no shadows, no paper texture, flat even lighting, photographed straight from above, high contrast, crisp detail.
