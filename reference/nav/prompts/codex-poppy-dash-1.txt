Four short, soft horizontal watercolour dashes in the warm orange of a California poppy, painted on plain white paper, stacked with wide white space between them. Each dash is one quick touch of a wet round brush: about twelve times as long as it is thick, slightly fuller at the left where the brush lands and tapering gently to a soft round point at the right, the colour pooling a little darker along its lower edge, with fine granulation. Clean soft wet edges, no dry-brush streaks, no splatter. Warm sunny poppy orange, not red, not brown. Pure white background, no paper texture, no shadow, no other marks, no text, no letters, no signature, no seal, no stamp. Flat even lighting, photographed straight from above, landscape picture.
