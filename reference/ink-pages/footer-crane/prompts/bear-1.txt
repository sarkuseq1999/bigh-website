Two reference paintings are attached. The FIRST is the bird that stands in our website's footer today, at rest in a tuft of grass: copy its brush hand and its layout (the same painter, the same sumi ink, the same bold few strokes, the same low tuft of loose sage-green grass blades with a faint pale sage wash on the ground, the same plain warm cream paper, the same size in the frame and the same empty paper margins). The SECOND is the California park sign this animal will sit beside on our website: copy its loose watercolour grass and its warm cream paper. Do NOT paint the sign, its posts or its poppy. Do NOT paint the crane.

Paint ONE young California black bear (a yearling cub, the kind that lives in California's state parks; California's flag carries a bear) in place of the crane, sitting in that same tuft of grass, seen from the side, facing left. It leans forward with its nose reaching out and down toward the lower left, sniffing a small wildflower growing just outside the picture on its left, at the height of the grass. Curious and gentle, at ease. Round ears, a short muzzle, its body one soft rounded mass. Anatomically correct: one head, two ears, four legs.

STYLE: a bold freehand ink painting with a little pale colour laid over it, like the first reference. The bear a few loaded, wet strokes of black and grey sumi ink, its fur the dry-brush edges of those strokes, never drawn hair by hair; its muzzle a touch of warm muted brown wash. Muted colours only, in the sign's quiet palette (ink, warm brown, sage); no bright colour. No fine detail, no stippling, no pen outlines, not a teddy bear, not a soft storybook illustration, not cute, not a cartoon.

Landscape format, wider than tall. The bear and its grass inside the frame, filling about two thirds of the frame's height, with clear empty paper margins on every side and nothing cut off; its nose stays inside the frame with paper to its left. The grass tuft fades out softly into the paper on both sides, no hard edge. No flower in the picture. Plain empty warm cream paper, evenly lit, no vignette. No sign, no post, no trees, no rocks, no mountains, no mist, no sun, no other animals. An American, California scene: no Chinese characters, no seals or stamps, no text, no signature, no watermark, no border. Nothing red, no gold.
