It is the hand to paint in: the hummingbird on our homepage, painted in a few wet washes. Copy its way of painting exactly: a handful of big, loaded, wet brush strokes, colour pooling and bleeding at the edges, soft blooms where wet paint meets wet paint, dry-brush streaks where the brush ran out, and bare paper left as the light. Only the head is a little more defined, as on the hummingbird.

WHAT TO PAINT: A young California black bear (a yearling cub, the kind that lives in California's state parks) sitting in a low tuft of sage-green grass, seen from the side, facing left, leaning forward with its nose reaching out and down toward the lower left, sniffing a small wildflower just outside the picture on its left, at the height of the grass.

A QUICK PAINTING: the whole picture in about a dozen brush strokes, as if painted in a minute. Edges soft and dissolving into the paper, or broken by the dry brush; never a dashed or dotted contour line running around the body, never hatched edges. Big areas of bare paper inside the animal, left as the light. Smooth paper and clean transparent washes: no speckled, mottled, grainy or digital-filter texture anywhere.

PAINT THE BEAR in about six or seven wet strokes of warm black and grey ink: one big loaded wash for the round back and haunch, one for the shoulder and front leg, the ink paler and bleeding where the light falls along its back (soft, never a stripe), dry-brush edges for the fur, two small soft round strokes for the ears, set clearly apart on top of its head. Its muzzle a touch of warm muted brown wash, the nose and eye single dark dots. No fur drawn hair by hair, no outline around the bear. Muted colours only (ink, warm brown, sage); no bright colour. Not a teddy bear, not cute, not a cartoon.

THE GRASS: a few quick sage-green blades and a faint pale sage wash on the ground under the bear, fading softly into the paper on both sides, painted in the same loose wet hand.

Landscape format, wider than tall. The bear and its grass inside the frame, filling about two thirds of the frame's height, with clear empty paper margins on every side and nothing cut off; its nose stays inside the frame with paper to its left. Plain empty warm cream paper, evenly lit, no vignette. No flower, no sign, no post, no trees, no rocks, no other animals, no hummingbird.
