It is the hand to paint in: the Big Sur painting at the end of our homepage, ink and wash. Copy its way of painting exactly: drawn with a sumi ink brush, lines that swell, thin and break, a few loaded dark accents and dry-brush strokes, then light, transparent colour washed over the ink, with bare paper left between.

WHAT TO PAINT: A young California black bear (a yearling cub, the kind that lives in California's state parks) sitting in a low tuft of sage-green grass, seen from the side, facing left, leaning forward with its nose reaching out and down toward the lower left, sniffing a small wildflower just outside the picture on its left, at the height of the grass.

A QUICK PAINTING: the whole picture in about a dozen brush strokes, as if painted in a minute. Edges soft and dissolving into the paper, or broken by the dry brush; never a dashed or dotted contour line running around the body, never hatched edges. Big areas of bare paper inside the animal, left as the light. Smooth paper and clean transparent washes: no speckled, mottled, grainy or digital-filter texture anywhere.

PAINT THE BEAR as a sumi ink painting: a few big loaded, wet strokes of black ink for the round body, the haunch and the front leg, the ink greying where it thins along the top of the back (soft, never a stripe), dry-brush strokes at the edges for the fur, two small soft round strokes for the ears, set clearly apart on top of its head. Then a pale warm brown wash laid loosely over the muzzle; the nose and eye single dark dots. No fur drawn hair by hair, no closed outline traced around the bear. Muted colours only (ink, warm brown, sage); no bright colour. Not a teddy bear, not cute, not a cartoon.

THE GRASS: a few quick ink-and-sage blades and a faint pale sage wash on the ground under the bear, fading softly into the paper on both sides, in the same hand.

Landscape format, wider than tall. The bear and its grass inside the frame, filling about two thirds of the frame's height, with clear empty paper margins on every side and nothing cut off; its nose stays inside the frame with paper to its left. Plain empty warm cream paper, evenly lit, no vignette. No flower, no sign, no post, no trees, no rocks, no sea, no hills, no people, no other animals. An American, California scene: no Chinese characters, no seals or stamps, no text, no signature, no watermark, no border. Nothing red, no gold.
