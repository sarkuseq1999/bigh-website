What it does

  • On a laptop, one painting fills the section and the words sit in its sky.
  • On a phone, the picture runs edge to edge, then the words follow.
  • Big screens get the same painting, kept to the window's height; small laptops push the painting down so the words never run into the bay.
  • His face is his real portrait. Only the landscape and the grass are new.
  • The four new lines are drafted in Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese and Japanese, like the other new NuriCell lines (not yet checked by a native speaker).
9 / 10
  • Holds it back: the hills read grey more than golden, and nothing moves yet. On a 1280 × 800 laptop the section runs a little past the screen, so you scroll a bit to see the caption.
