Six power plants

One quick sample painting for each idea, beside the real Mitochondria words, the way it would sit on the homepage. The three new ones, D, E and F, come first; A, B and C from before are below. The one you pick would carry Mitochondria and Free radicals; Aging cells keeps the brain cell you picked.

My pick

D. Inside the powerhouse

What are mitochondria—and why do they matter?

A simple guide to your cells’ tiny power plants, their role in everyday energy, and what mitochondrial health means.

  • Inside most of your cells
  • Turn food into usable energy
  • A small part with a big job

Free radicals

  • Closer on the furnace: the fire throws off sparks, and a screen in the chimney catches most of them.

Why I pick it: it shows how the power is made, step by step, the way your cells turn food into energy. Fuel in, fire, wheel, power out, all in one look. It's the most like a storybook. The worry: it has the most parts, so the real one must stay this simple.

My honest take
  • These are quick samples, one try each. The one you pick gets polished, and painted again for Free radicals.
