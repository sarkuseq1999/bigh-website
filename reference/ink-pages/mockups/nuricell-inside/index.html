What I tidied

  1. The small grey "What's inside" is gone. The big heading already says it, so it only looked lost.
  2. "Per serving of 3 capsules" moved up, right under the heading. The heading says "every capsule", but the amounts are for 3 capsules. Readers need that before the numbers, not after.
  3. The other ingredients sit in one small label card, like the label on a pressed-plant sheet.
  4. The note "research is about the nutrients, not the product" moves to the end of the chapter, joined with the one already there. Same honesty, said once.
