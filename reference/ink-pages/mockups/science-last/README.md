# Science zoom: the last picture (November 12, 2026)

Mo found the picture the zoom pulls back to (the grandfather and grandchild walking away) "a
little boring". Round 1 kept the pair (A a gold-leaf kite, B grandpa swinging the child round);
round 2, "think freely", tried anything (A a climber in Yosemite, B a rower at sunrise with
gold-leaf ripples, C swing dancing in Golden Gate Park). Mo picked round 2's A, the climber.

- `review/index.html`: the page Mo saw (an Artifact), with desktop and phone shots.
- `shoot.py` + `choices.json`: swap a plate into the public demo's last view and shoot it (nothing
  in `src`); `film.py` films the kite's sway; `review.py` makes the page's JPGs.
- `gild_kite.py`, `gild_ochre.py`: lay the site's gold leaf on a painting's kite or ochre strokes.

Only the climber's paintings stay in `reference/ink-pages/science/originals/` (`end-climb-3`,
`tall-end-climb-3-1`, and its hand `climb-hand-1`, `tall-climb-hand-1-1`); the other takes are in
`bigh-archive\science-last-unused-2026-11-12\`, so the scripts above no longer find them.
