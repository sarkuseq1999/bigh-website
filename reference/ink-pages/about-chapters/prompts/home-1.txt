STYLE: A landscape painting for a California brand's website, in loose ink and thin watercolour on plain warm white paper. Use the attached paintings only as the reference for the brush hand (do not paint what they show): the same painter, the same few wet washes with soft bleeding edges, a few dry-brush streaks, very few strokes, lots of bare paper. The more it looks like a painting, the better. Not photo-real, not a detailed drawing, not a storybook illustration, no pen outlines. Mostly soft grey ink, with only a thin touch of the colours named below.

It is an American, California scene in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters, NOT an Asian ink painting: no pagoda, no bamboo, no twisted pine on a rock, no misty peaks with waterfalls, no characters, no seal or stamp.

COMPOSITION: Landscape, 1536 x 1024. A quiet Southern California morning.
- The upper half is bare paper sky, very pale, no clouds, no birds, no sun.
- Far back, across the middle of the picture, a long soft range of blue-grey mountains, half lost in morning haze, like the San Gabriel Mountains seen from the valley.
- On the far left, three tall slender California fan palms, their trunks one thin grey stroke each, their heads quick dark grey-green dabs, rising above the mountains. On the far right, two smaller palms further back.
- The lower third is a wide, flat, open stretch of pale golden-grey ground, one or two broad soft washes, with a few dry-brush strokes of golden grass at its far edge.
- IMPORTANT: the centre of the lower third, about 70% of the picture's width, must be EMPTY, even and quiet: nothing standing there, no rocks, no plants, no path, no marks. Objects will be placed standing in a row there later.

NEVER: no people, no animals, no buildings, no houses, no fences, no roads, no cars, no text, letters, numbers or writing anywhere, no signature, no lab glassware, no pills, no bottles, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo, no sparkle.
