A minimal hand-painted sumi-e ink and watercolour painting on warm cream paper, for the footer of a California wellness brand's website. Wide landscape image.

Subject: a trail sign reduced to a few brushstrokes, the way an ink painter would suggest it in seconds. The plank is ONE broad horizontal stroke of a wide flat brush loaded with pale warm-grey ink wash, dragged once across the paper from left to right: its top and bottom edges slightly ragged with dry-brush texture, a little darker where the brush first touched down at the left, the right end trailing off in dry-brush streaks. Two posts are two short vertical strokes of darker grey ink below the plank, their tops just showing above it. At the foot of the right post, a small tuft of wild California grasses and one California poppy, the poppy's petals a single soft pale-gold watercolour touch, its stem and leaves in sage green. A light wash of ground mist at the foot of the posts, fading to bare paper.

The middle of the broad plank stroke is evenly toned and COMPLETELY BLANK: a quiet pale wash, light enough that dark charcoal text on it will read clearly. No letters, numbers, marks or symbols; text is added later by the website. The plank stroke spans about the middle 65 percent of the image width and is tall enough to hold two lines of text.

How it must be painted: confident, loose, wet; very few strokes; lots of empty paper. NO pen outlines, NO contour lines, no wood grain, no detail, no storybook or cartoon look. Soft wet edges that bleed into the paper.

Palette: soft grey ink, sage green and warm cream like the attached picture, with one touch of pale gold for the poppy. Muted and calm. No gold leaf, no sun, no orange, no red.

Composition: flat front view, no perspective, no cast shadow. Generous plain cream paper all around; the same flat cream everywhere, no vignette, no paper edge, no frame. Use the attached picture only for its brushwork and colour; do not copy its pine, cliffs, cranes, mountains or sun.

This is an American, California scene: no Chinese characters, no seals or stamps, no red seal, no scrolls. Absolutely no text, letters, signature, seal or watermark anywhere in the image.
