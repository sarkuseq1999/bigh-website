A hand-painted ink-and-wash illustration on warm cream rice paper, for the footer of a California wellness brand's website. Use the attached sketch only for the composition.

Subject: a rustic wooden trail sign of the kind found in California state parks and US national parks, seen perfectly straight on. One wide, horizontal, thick plank of dark brown redwood with softly rounded, routed edges, mounted on two short square wooden posts; the tops of the posts rise a little above the plank. The front of the plank has one long recessed panel with a routed border. That panel is COMPLETELY BLANK: an even, quiet, dark warm-brown wash with only very faint horizontal grain, no knots, no marks, no letters, no numbers, no symbols. Text will be added later by the website, so the panel must stay empty and evenly toned from end to end.

Setting: tufts of dry golden California grass painted loosely around the foot of the posts and along the ground line. To the right of the sign, clearly separated from it by a gap of empty paper, a low rolling hill with one California valley oak, small and quiet. Nothing to the left of the sign except a few blades of grass.

Style: confident black ink brush outlines with dry-brush texture, layered warm brown-grey washes for the wood, muted golden ochre for the grass, grey-brown for the oak. Elegant and minimal, the same family as a Chinese ink painting but clearly an American landscape. Flat front elevation, no perspective, no cast shadow on the background.

Composition: the sign fills about the middle half of the image width; generous plain cream paper all around it, the same flat warm cream everywhere, no vignette, no paper edge, no frame. Absolutely no text, letters, signature, seal or watermark anywhere.
