COMPOSITION: Landscape, 1536 x 1024. The plant stands large in the front, in the left half, painted closest and darkest; the place it grows lies behind it, smaller and paler, with a lot of quiet pale sky. The painting fades into bare paper at every edge: no frame, no border, no hard rectangle. It must still read at once when shown small, about 300 pixels wide: one big plant shape, one simple place.

THE PLACE: a vineyard in late summer: rows of vines running in gentle curves over a slope toward low hills, a pale sky. No buildings, no barrels, no glasses, no bottles.

THE PLANT, in front: a hanging cluster of dark blue-purple wine grapes on the vine with two or three big grape leaves. The grapes are a few overlapping wet circles bleeding into each other, not every grape drawn.

COLOURS: thin grey-violet on the grapes, pale grey-green leaves and vine rows, a faint warm ochre on the slope.

STYLE: A painting for a California brand's website, in loose ink and thin watercolour on plain warm white paper. Use the attached paintings only as the reference for the brush hand (do not paint what they show): the same painter, the same few wet washes with soft bleeding edges, a few dry-brush streaks, very few strokes, lots of bare paper. The more it looks like a painting, the better. Not photo-real, not a detailed botanical drawing, not a storybook illustration, no pen outlines.

INK FIRST, COLOUR SECOND: paint it mostly in soft grey sumi ink washes, like the attached oak, with only thin, pale touches of the colours named below laid over the ink. Fewer strokes than you think. Big soft shapes that bleed at the edge, nothing outlined, no fine veins, no speckle or grain. If a part can be left to bare paper, leave it.

It is in the spirit of an American naturalist's field sketchbook, NOT an Asian ink painting: no scroll, no pagoda, no bamboo, no twisted pine on a rock, no characters, no seal or stamp.

NEVER: no people, no hands, no text, letters, numbers or writing anywhere, no signature, no lab glassware, no pills or capsules, no powder, no jars or bottles, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo, no sparkle, no gold.
