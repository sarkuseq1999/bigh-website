One simple, everyday ceramic mug with a round handle on its right, the kind on any American kitchen table in the morning, seen from the front and a little from above, filled with hot coffee: the mug a few confident grey strokes with a darker rim and a soft shadow, the coffee a warm grey wash. One fine, graceful wisp of steam rises from it in a pale grey stroke. In front of the mug, a little to the left, on the same level, lies one single capsule painted in soft real gold leaf (the capsule is yellow), with a fine grey outline and a small soft shadow. Nothing else. Quiet morning calm.
