STYLE: An ink-and-wash painting on plain white paper, in the spirit of the plein-air watercolour painters. The land is first drawn and shaded with a brush in grey and black ink, quickly and loosely, with dry-brush strokes; then a few light, transparent watercolour washes are laid over it. The sky is one big wet wash with no ink in it. The ink does the work on the land; the colour is light and calm. Lots of bare paper. You can see every brush stroke; soft bleeding edges where the paper was wet, crisp ones where it was dry. Richness comes from brushwork and ink, never from fine or photographic detail. The more it looks like a painting by a grown-up painter, the better. Not photo-real, no smooth digital gradients, no pen outlines, not a storybook or greeting-card illustration, not a cartoon. A Brazilian countryside scene in Minas Gerais, NOT an Asian ink painting: no misty peaks, no bands of fog, no pine tree clinging to a rock, no cranes, no boats, no temples.

THE PICTURE: the opening painting of a product page: the rolling hills of Minas Gerais, Brazil, on a clear dry-season morning, where bees gather resin from a wild shrub called alecrim; one golden ipê tree in full yellow bloom stands on a far hill; and almost all of it is SKY. Large dark lettering and a product will be laid over it later, so the painting must stay simple, calm and light. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

COMPOSITION, top to bottom (keep these heights):
- From the top edge down to 69% of the height: open sky (a very low horizon: the land is only a thin band). One big wet wash of clear soft blue, the blue of a cloudless dry-season morning: mid-pale at the top, much paler and a touch warmer toward the land, uneven as a real wash is, with granulation, brush direction showing, and one or two soft backruns. Two small soft clouds left as bare white paper, high up near the top corners only. No sun, no birds, no hard-edged clouds.
- From 69% to 72%: far, LOW rolling hills across the whole width, a thin band, their top a gentle, low line that never rises above 69% of the height: light washes of soft green and pale straw-gold grass with a little soft blue in the farthest ridge, a thin track of warm red earth winding over one slope. On a far hill at about 78% across stands ONE ipê tree in full bloom, small, its round crown a loose dab of bright yellow wash with no leaves, its trunk a few grey ink strokes. Two or three small white wooden beehive boxes far off on a slope at about 20% across, tiny, a few strokes each. Calm and pale.
- From 72% to 78%: the nearest ground, in the foreground, a thin band. From the left edge to 40% across and from 60% across to the right edge: low, bushy alecrim shrubs, LOW: no shrub rises more than 5% of the picture's height, their many slender upright stems drawn quickly in grey ink, covered in small narrow needle-like leaves laid as loose fresh-green and olive washes, with a few soft cream-white touches of tiny flowers at their tips. In the very middle of the picture (from 42% to 58% across) the ground is flat, low and bare, its level at 77% of the height, only short grass and a little warm red earth: an EMPTY spot where an object will be placed later. Nothing stands there, no shrub, no stone.
- From 78% to 81%: the grass thins out downward into a few loose dry-brush strokes and specks.
- The bottom 19% of the picture: completely bare white paper. Nothing there.

EDGES: the sky, the hills and the shrubs run off the left and right edges, and the sky off the top edge, with no fade, no vignette and no border. The bottom ends in bare white paper, never in a straight painted line.

NEVER: no text, letters or numbers anywhere, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold paint or gold leaf, no bottles, jars, glasses or products, no pills, capsules or powder, no people, no animals, no bees drawn as dots, no birds, no buildings other than the tiny hive boxes, no fence, no road, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo, no sun disc.
