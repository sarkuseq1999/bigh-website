One simple, everyday ceramic mug with a round handle on its right, the kind on any American kitchen table in the morning, seen from the front and a little from above, filled with hot tea: the mug a few confident grey strokes with a darker rim and a soft shadow, the tea a pale warm wash. One fine, graceful wisp of steam rises from it in a pale grey stroke. In front of the mug, a little to the left, lying on its side on the same level, is one single dietary-supplement capsule: a small, smooth, elongated oblong pill made of two rounded halves that meet in the middle, like a vitamin capsule, never a coffee pod or a cup shape, painted in soft real gold leaf (the capsule is yellow) with a fine grey outline and a small soft shadow. Nothing else. Quiet morning calm.
