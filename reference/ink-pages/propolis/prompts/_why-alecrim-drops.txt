One branch of alecrim, a Brazilian shrub, rising diagonally from the lower left to the upper right: slender, slightly woody stems in confident black ink, and many small, narrow, needle-like leaves in quick grey and black strokes, gathered in soft tufts at the shoot tips. At the very tip of five or six shoots sits one small, round, glistening bead of sticky resin made of real gold leaf, like a drop of amber. One honeybee, only a few quick strokes (a dark striped body, pale grey wash wings), hovers beside the highest shoot tip, a tiny gold pellet of resin on its hind leg. Calm, light, airy.
