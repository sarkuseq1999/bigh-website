A quiet, wide Brazilian plain in Minas Gerais in the dry season: one faint, soft horizon line in pale grey wash across the lower third of the picture, a few low tufts of dry grass along it. On the right third, standing alone on the horizon, one small yellow ipê tree (Brazil's national tree) in full bloom: a slender, twisting dark ink trunk and branches, its whole round crown a cloud of blossoms in real gold leaf. Everything else is open, empty paper: the left two-thirds of the plain is bare, where an object will stand. Calm, spacious, very sparse. No sun, no other trees, no buildings, no people, no animals.
