A small, shallow, round porcelain dish, seen from slightly above, painted in a few pale grey strokes with a darker rim, filling the middle of the picture. In the dish lie three or four small pieces of raw green propolis: sticky plant resin that glows like amber, painted in clear, luminous washes of olive-green-gold ink with real gold leaf glinting along their edges and bright highlights where the light passes through them; precious and clean, like small pieces of amber or jade, never like dirt, soil, rock or ore. Beside the dish on the right, lying on the same level, one vegetarian capsule, a few confident pale grey strokes with a fine darker outline and a small soft shadow, no gold on it. Nothing else. Simple, quiet, precious.
