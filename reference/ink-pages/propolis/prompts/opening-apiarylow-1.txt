STYLE: An ink-and-wash painting on plain white paper, in the spirit of the plein-air watercolour painters. The land is first drawn and shaded with a brush in grey and black ink, quickly and loosely, with dry-brush strokes; then a few light, transparent watercolour washes are laid over it. The sky is one big wet wash with no ink in it. The ink does the work on the land; the colour is light and calm. Lots of bare paper. You can see every brush stroke; soft bleeding edges where the paper was wet, crisp ones where it was dry. Richness comes from brushwork and ink, never from fine or photographic detail. The more it looks like a painting by a grown-up painter, the better. Not photo-real, no smooth digital gradients, no pen outlines, not a storybook or greeting-card illustration, not a cartoon. A Brazilian countryside scene in Minas Gerais, NOT an Asian ink painting: no misty peaks, no bands of fog, no pine tree clinging to a rock, no cranes, no boats, no temples.

THE PICTURE: the opening painting of a product page: a beekeeper's hillside in Minas Gerais, Brazil, on a warm late-afternoon: a short row of white wooden beehive boxes standing among wild alecrim shrubs, the shrub the bees gather their green resin from, low hills behind; and almost all of it is SKY. Large dark lettering and a product will be laid over it later, so the painting must stay simple, calm and light. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

COMPOSITION, top to bottom (keep these heights):
- From the top edge down to 69% of the height: open sky (a very low horizon: the land is only a thin band). One big wet wash of soft clear blue, mid-pale at the top, much paler and warmer, a touch of pale apricot, toward the land, uneven as a real wash is, with granulation, brush direction showing, and one or two soft backruns. Two small soft clouds left as bare white paper, high up near the top corners only. No sun, no birds, no hard-edged clouds.
- From 69% to 72%: far, LOW rolling hills across the whole width, a thin band in pale grey-green and soft blue washes, their top a gentle, low line that never rises above 69% of the height. Calm and pale.
- From 72% to 78%: the hillside in front, a thin band. From the left edge to 40% across: three small white wooden beehive boxes on short stands, LOW (no taller than 4% of the picture's height), in a loose row going away, drawn in a few grey ink strokes with their white left as bare paper and a little warm shadow, half hidden among shrubs. From 60% across to the right edge: low, bushy alecrim shrubs, LOW: no shrub rises more than 5% of the picture's height, their many slender upright stems drawn quickly in grey ink, covered in small narrow needle-like leaves laid as loose fresh-green and olive washes, a few soft cream-white touches of tiny flowers at their tips. More alecrim around the hives. In the very middle of the picture (from 42% to 58% across) the ground is flat, low and bare, its level at 77% of the height, only short grass and a little warm red earth: an EMPTY spot where an object will be placed later. Nothing stands there.
- From 78% to 81%: the grass thins out downward into a few loose dry-brush strokes and specks.
- The bottom 19% of the picture: completely bare white paper. Nothing there.

EDGES: the sky, the hills and the shrubs run off the left and right edges, and the sky off the top edge, with no fade, no vignette and no border. The bottom ends in bare white paper, never in a straight painted line.

NEVER: no text, letters or numbers anywhere, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold paint or gold leaf, no bottles, jars, glasses or products, no pills, capsules or powder, no people, no beekeeper, no bees drawn as dots or specks, no birds, no animals, no buildings, no fence, no road, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo, no sun disc.
