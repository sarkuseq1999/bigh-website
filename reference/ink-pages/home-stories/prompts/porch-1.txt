THE STORY BESIDE IT: "Weekday mornings are hectic in our house. I keep my supplement with my breakfast things so I remember it."

THE SUBJECT: Breakfast on the back porch of a California family home, early on a weekday morning. A small weathered wooden table on a plank porch, its near corner running off the left edge of the picture. On it, loosely grouped: a white enamel coffee mug with a handle and a thin wisp of steam; a tall glass of orange juice; a shallow bowl heaped with California oranges, a few leaves still on one; a small glass jam jar with three or four California poppies; a child's metal lunch box with its lid shut and a set of car keys beside it. Behind the table, one plain porch rail, and beyond it, far off and very pale, rolling golden California hills with a single valley oak on one of them, all in two or three wet washes between 26% and 42% down, fading into the paper at the sides. Low morning sun from the right: long soft shadows across the table.

COLOUR: the oranges and the poppies a thin warm orange (the brightest note, but still light and transparent), the far hills a pale dry-grass gold, the oak a soft sage grey, the lunch box a faded sage green, the table and porch warm grey, everything else soft grey ink. No gold leaf.
