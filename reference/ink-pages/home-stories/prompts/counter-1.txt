THE STORY BESIDE IT: "Weekday mornings are hectic in our house. I keep my supplement with my breakfast things so I remember it." A real photograph of the supplement bottle will be placed into this painting afterwards, standing among the breakfast things; do NOT paint any bottle.

THE SUBJECT: A busy weekday morning on the kitchen counter of a California family home. A plain wooden counter top seen from the front and slightly above, its edge running off the left side of the picture; behind it, only a pale wall wash and the edge of a sunny window at the top right. On the counter, loosely grouped: a white enamel coffee mug with a handle and a thin wisp of steam; a shallow bowl heaped with California oranges, a few leaves still on one; a small glass jam jar with three or four California poppies; a child's metal lunch box with its lid shut; a set of car keys. Morning sun from the right: a pale warm wash of light and soft shadows.

THE EMPTY SPOT (important): leave a clear gap of bare counter where the bottle will stand, centred at 52% across, its base on the counter at 80% down. The gap is 16% of the picture wide and runs from 50% down to 82% down. Nothing in it at all: no object, no shadow, no stroke; the objects stand to its left and right, none in front of it.

COLOUR: the oranges and the poppies a thin warm orange (the brightest note, but still light and transparent), the lunch box a faded sage green, the counter warm grey and pale honey, everything else soft grey ink. No gold.
