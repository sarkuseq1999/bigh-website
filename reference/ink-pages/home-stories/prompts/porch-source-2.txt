THE STORY BESIDE IT: "I'd heard of propolis, but I didn't know what made the green kind different. Reading about its source in Minas Gerais, Brazil, helped me understand what I was buying." (Green propolis is the resin bees gather from a Brazilian shrub with narrow leaves, Baccharis dracunculifolia, called alecrim-do-campo.)
