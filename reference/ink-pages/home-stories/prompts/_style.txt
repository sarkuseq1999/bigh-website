STYLE: A quiet still life for a California brand's website, in ink and thin watercolour on plain warm white paper. Use the attached paintings only as the reference for the brush hand (do not paint what they show): the same painter. The objects are first drawn and shaded quickly and loosely with a brush in grey and black ink, the way the attached coast is drawn; then a few light, transparent watercolour washes are laid over them, soft and wet like the attached mailbox. The ink does the work; the colour is light and calm. A few dry-brush streaks, soft bleeding edges where the paper was wet, crisp ones where it was dry, lots of bare paper. The more it looks like a painting, the better. Not photo-real, no fine detail everywhere, no smooth digital gradients, not a detailed drawing, not a storybook illustration, no pen outlines.

It is an American, California home, in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters, NOT an Asian ink painting: no tea bowls or tea cups without handles, no blossom branch in a vase, no low table, no sliced pear, no bamboo, no inkstone, no calligraphy brush, no scroll, no folding screen, no paper lantern, no twisted pine, no characters, no seal or stamp.

COMPOSITION: Square, 1024 x 1024. It sits beside the words of a customer's short story on a website; the left 15% of the picture is often cut off by the edge of the screen, and all four edges are faded into the page.
- The top third is mostly bare paper, with at most pale washes of light.
- The objects sit in the lower middle: the main ones between 22% and 80% across and between 40% and 86% down.
- Everything dissolves softly into bare paper towards every edge; no hard edge, no frame.

NEVER: no people, no hands, no faces, no pets, no text, letters, numbers or writing anywhere (not on any packet, book, jar or box), no logos, no brand labels, no bottles of pills, no pills or capsules, no powder, no lab glassware, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo, no sparkle.
