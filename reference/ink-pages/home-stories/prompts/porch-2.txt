THE STORY BESIDE IT: "Weekday mornings are hectic in our house. I keep my supplement with my breakfast things so I remember it."

The first attached picture is the first draft of this painting. Keep its hand, its colours and its light; change what is described below. The other attached pictures are only the reference for the brush hand.

THE SUBJECT: Breakfast on the back porch of a California family home, early on a weekday morning. A small weathered wooden table on a plank porch; behind it one plain porch rail. Beyond the rail, rolling golden California hills in the morning sun, nearer and larger than in the draft: the hills rise to 14% down at the right and fill the picture from 14% to 46% down, two or three wet washes of dry-grass gold and pale sage, paler where they are farther away. On the nearest hill, at about 62% across, one big valley oak, its broad round crown about 30% of the picture wide, its top at about 8% down: a few wet washes of soft sage grey and a dark ink trunk. The hills fade into the paper at the left edge.

On the table, loosely grouped, the main objects between 24% and 84% across and between 50% and 86% down: a white enamel coffee mug with a handle and a thin wisp of steam; a tall glass of orange juice; a shallow bowl heaped with California oranges, a few leaves still on one; a small glass jam jar with three or four California poppies; a brown paper lunch bag standing upright, its top folded over twice; a set of car keys beside it. Nothing important left of 24% across: the left edge is only the table top running off the picture. Low morning sun from the right: long soft shadows across the table.

COLOUR: the oranges and the poppies a thin warm orange (the brightest note, but still light and transparent), the hills dry-grass gold and pale sage, the oak soft sage grey, the paper bag pale kraft brown, the table and porch warm grey, everything else soft grey ink. No gold leaf.
