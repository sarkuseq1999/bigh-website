THE STORY BESIDE IT: "Weekday mornings are hectic in our house. I keep my supplement with my breakfast things so I remember it."

THE SUBJECT: A busy weekday morning on the kitchen table of a California family home. A plain wooden farmhouse table seen from slightly above, its near corner running off the left edge of the picture. On it, loosely grouped: a white enamel coffee mug with a handle and a thin wisp of steam; a tall glass of orange juice; a shallow bowl heaped with California oranges and one lemon, a few leaves still on one orange; a small glass jam jar with three or four California poppies; a child's metal lunch box with its lid shut; a set of car keys dropped beside it. Morning sun comes from the right through a window you cannot see: a pale warm wash of light across the table and the long soft shadow of a window frame falling on it.

COLOUR: the oranges and the poppies a thin warm orange (the brightest note, but still light and transparent), the lemon a pale yellow, the lunch box a faded sage green, the table warm grey and pale honey, everything else soft grey ink. No gold.
