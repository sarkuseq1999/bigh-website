THE STORY BESIDE IT: "Weekday mornings are hectic in our house. I keep my supplement with my breakfast things so I remember it."

The first attached picture is the first draft of this painting. Keep its hand, its colours and its light; change what is described below. The other attached pictures are only the reference for the brush hand.

THE SUBJECT: A busy weekday morning in the kitchen of a California family home. A plain wooden farmhouse table pushed against a big kitchen window. Through the window, in the morning sun, the backyard lemon tree, heavy with lemons: a few loose wet washes of sage green, the lemons quick dabs of pale yellow, no outlines; the window frame is only a few grey strokes and runs off the top edge. The window fills the upper part of the picture, from 4% to 52% down and from 28% across to the right edge; the window glass is mostly bare paper with the tree in it.

On the table, loosely grouped, the main objects between 24% and 84% across and between 48% and 86% down: a white enamel coffee mug with a handle and a thin wisp of steam; a tall glass of orange juice; a shallow bowl heaped with California oranges, a few leaves still on one; a small glass jam jar with three or four California poppies; a brown paper lunch bag standing upright, its top folded over twice; a set of car keys dropped beside it. Nothing important left of 24% across: the left edge is only the table top running off the picture. Morning sun from the window: a pale warm wash of light across the table and soft shadows falling towards us.

COLOUR: the oranges and the poppies a thin warm orange (the brightest note, but still light and transparent), the lemons pale yellow, the lemon tree soft sage, the paper bag pale kraft brown, the table warm grey and pale honey, everything else soft grey ink. No gold.
