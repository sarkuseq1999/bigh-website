Do not paint the crane; paint only this subject in its hand: a small historic brick powerhouse from about 1905 seen with its front wall left open, like a storybook cutaway, so we see the one simple idea inside, left to right: at the left, a worker (small, seen from behind) feeds a split log into the round open door of a furnace; the fire inside the furnace is real gold leaf; above it a plump boiler gives a soft puff of white steam through a pipe to the right, where a big spoked wheel turns (a few curved motion strokes around its rim); from the wheel, two thin wires leave the building at the right and run off on a wooden pole, and a fine thread of gold runs along the first span of wire. The only gold in the picture is the fire and that thread. The building's brick walls, roof and floor are a few pale washes; everything inside is a few confident strokes, large and simple, readable at a glance.
