A small stone mortar with its pestle resting in it, seen from the front and a little from above, filling the middle of the picture, painted in a few broad grey wet strokes with a darker rim and a soft shadow at its foot. Beside it on the right, lying on the same level, three loose marigold petals and one small grape leaf, each a single stroke. Sumi ink in black and grey; the petals a soft golden-yellow wash (paint, not gold leaf). No gold leaf. Quiet, careful, practical.
