Brush calligraphy of numbers only, written in one row across the middle of the paper in sumi ink by a master's steady, controlled hand: the number 2, then a small multiplication sign, then the number 60, then a small equals sign, then the number 120. Read exactly as: 2 × 60 = 120 . Each numeral bold, refined and perfectly legible (clear Western numerals, never stylised into other shapes), with wet strokes and a little dry-brush at the ends; the two signs smaller and lighter, with a clear gap between every number and sign. Nothing else on the paper: no other characters, no words, no gold.
