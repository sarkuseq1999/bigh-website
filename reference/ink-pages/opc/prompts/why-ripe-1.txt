STYLE: An ink-and-watercolour painting on plain warm white paper, in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters. The subject is first drawn and shaded with a brush in grey and black ink, quickly and loosely, with dry-brush strokes; then a few light, transparent watercolour washes are laid over it. Lots of bare paper. You can see every brush stroke; soft bleeding edges where the paper was wet, crisp ones where it was dry. Richness comes from brushwork and ink, never from fine or photographic detail. The more it looks like a painting by a grown-up painter, the better. Not photo-real, no smooth digital gradients, no pen outlines, not a botanical-textbook plate, not a storybook or greeting-card illustration, not a cartoon. NOT an Asian ink painting: no rice paper, no calligraphy, no red seal.

THE PICTURE: one chapter painting of a product page about antioxidants from grapes. Portrait, 1024 x 1536.

One generous bunch of ripe California red-wine grapes (a Cabernet bunch at harvest) hanging from a short piece of weathered brown vine cane at the top, with two or three broad grape leaves and one curling tendril. The bunch fills the middle of the picture from about 20% to 90% of the height and about 25% to 75% of the width; the leaves spread out above it to about 10% and 90% of the width.
- The grapes: deep violet-purple to blue-black, with the pale dusty bloom real grapes have, painted as loose wet dabs that run into one another; the bunch reads as ONE shape with round touches only at its edges, a little lighter where the late-summer light falls from the upper left, darker on its shadow side. Never a field of separate identical dots, never shiny.
- The leaves: broad sage and olive green washes over loose grey ink, their veins a few dry strokes; one leaf just beginning to turn a warm yellow at its edge.
- The cane and the tendril: grey-brown dry brush.
Generous bare paper on every side. The bottom 8% of the picture is bare paper.

NEVER: no text, letters or numbers, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold paint, no glow, no halo, no light shining through the fruit, no haze or wash of colour around the bunch, no background, no ground, no table, no sky, no bottles, glasses or capsules, no people, no animals, no red or rust clustered dots.
