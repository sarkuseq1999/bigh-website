A California vineyard at sunrise, seen from a low hill: four or five long rows of grapevines run from the foreground away toward a soft ridge of low rolling hills, the vines a few loose dry-brush strokes each, the rows simplified and calm, the hills one or two pale grey washes. Above the ridge, the rising sun as one round disc of real gold leaf, with a faint warm gold haze on the paper around it and a soft gold light caught along the top of the nearest vine row. No buildings, no people, no trees but the vines. Mostly empty paper for the sky. Calm, early, Californian.
