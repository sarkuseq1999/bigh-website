Six things from the plants in a formula, lying on the paper seen from straight above in two calm rows of three, each painted small and simply in a few loose strokes, with generous paper around and between them and nothing touching. Top row, left to right: a small bunch of dark grapes with one leaf; a curled piece of rough pine bark; three bilberries (small round dark berries) with a leaf. Bottom row, left to right: two tea leaves; one round slice of orange showing its segments; one marigold flower with layered petals. Sumi ink in black and grey, with a little soft colour wash only where the thing is coloured: a deep purple-grey for the grapes and berries, a warm orange wash for the orange slice, a golden-yellow wash for the marigold (paint, not gold leaf). No gold leaf. Quiet, orderly, botanical.
