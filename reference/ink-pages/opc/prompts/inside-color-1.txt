STYLE: An ink-and-watercolour painting on plain warm white paper, in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters. The subject is first drawn and shaded with a brush in grey and black ink, quickly and loosely, with dry-brush strokes; then a few light, transparent watercolour washes are laid over it. Lots of bare paper. You can see every brush stroke; soft bleeding edges where the paper was wet, crisp ones where it was dry. Richness comes from brushwork and ink, never from fine or photographic detail. The more it looks like a painting by a grown-up painter, the better. Not photo-real, no smooth digital gradients, no pen outlines, not a botanical-textbook plate, not a storybook or greeting-card illustration, not a cartoon. NOT an Asian ink painting: no rice paper, no calligraphy, no red seal.

THE PICTURE: the "What's inside" painting of a product page whose formula draws on many plants. Square, 1024 x 1024. Seen from straight above, on bare paper.

Six things from the plants, in two calm rows of three, each painted in a few loose strokes with real colour, generous bare paper around and between them, nothing touching, each about the same size (about a quarter of the picture's width).
Top row, left to right: a small bunch of ripe dark violet California wine grapes with one sage-green grape leaf; a curled piece of rough, warm grey-brown pine bark; three small round blue-black bilberries with the pale bloom of real berries, on a short twig with two small green leaves.
Bottom row, left to right: two fresh green tea leaves on a short stem; one round slice of orange showing its segments, warm orange and pale; one marigold flower with layered golden-orange petals and two leaves.
Quiet, orderly, a painter's study of plants, in the same hand as the rest.

NEVER: no text, letters or numbers, no labels, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold paint or gold leaf, no glow, no halo, no haze or wash of colour around anything, no background, no table, no basket, no ground, no shadows beyond a faint touch under each thing, no bottles, glasses, cups, jars or capsules, no powder, no pills, no people, no animals, no red or rust clustered dots.
