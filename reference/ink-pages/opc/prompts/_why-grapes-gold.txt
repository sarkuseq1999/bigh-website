One generous bunch of ripe grapes hanging from a short piece of vine with two broad grape leaves and one curling tendril, filling the picture from top to bottom, painted in a few confident wet strokes of black and grey sumi ink: each grape one round wet dab, the leaves broad loose washes with dry-brush edges. The bunch is lit from inside: every grape glows with real gold leaf at its heart, as if sunlight shone through the fruit, the gold brightest in the middle of the bunch and softer toward its edges, with a faint warm gold haze on the paper around the bunch. No other colour. Calm, ripe, luminous.
