COMPOSITION: Landscape, 1536 x 1024. The end of an old wooden California fruit crate, seen straight on, filling about 80% of the frame, standing very slightly tilted. Its three rough pine boards are a few warm grey-brown washes, the grain only hinted by two or three dry-brush streaks, a couple of dark nail heads. Bare paper all around the crate.

Pasted across the boards is a big paper label in the spirit of the old California orange-crate labels of the 1920s, but painted loosely by hand in ink and thin watercolour, NOT printed and NOT graphic: in the label, a valley floor with rows of round orange trees (soft green-grey domes, with only a few oranges, each large and soft; never a cluster of small dots), golden foothills behind them, and the snowy Sierra Nevada at the back in pale blue-grey. A round sun sits just above the mountains, laid in flat matte gold leaf. Across the top of the label runs an empty curling ribbon banner, left as plain cream paper with NOTHING written in it. One corner of the label is a little worn.

The banner, the label and the crate carry no letters, words, numbers, logos or brand names at all.
