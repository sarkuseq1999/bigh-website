COMPOSITION: Portrait, 1024 x 1536. A sunlit white stucco wall in Southern California in the morning, seen straight on and filling the frame, its plaster a few warm, very pale washes with a little texture from a dry brush. Across the wall falls the soft blue-grey shadow of a fan palm's fronds, coming in from the upper right corner, painted in a few loose, wet strokes (the shadow only; the palm itself is not in the picture).

In the middle of the wall, straight on and upright, a plain wooden clipboard hangs from one nail. Its clip at the top is laid in flat matte gold leaf. On it, a sheet of cream paper with three empty square boxes down its left side, evenly spaced, each box followed by one short, faint grey pencil line (a line only: no letters, no words, no numbers). The three boxes are EMPTY: nothing is ticked or written in them.

At the foot of the wall, to one side, a small terracotta pot with a little green succulent, and a narrow strip of warm ground. Bare paper at the frame's edges.
