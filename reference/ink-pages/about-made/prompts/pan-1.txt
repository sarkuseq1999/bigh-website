COMPOSITION: Landscape, 1536 x 1024. A Gold Rush prospector's gold pan resting at the edge of a clear Sierra Nevada creek, seen from a little above. The pan is wide, round and shallow, dark grey iron, painted in a few soft ink washes, and fills about half of the frame, a little left of centre. In it: a thin film of clear water, a little dark sand, and a few small flakes and specks of gold caught along the pan's inner ridge, laid in flat matte gold leaf (small, few, no shine).

Around it: smooth pale granite stones, the creek's shallow water as a few pale blue-grey washes over pebbles, one sprig of sage-green at the water's edge. At the top of the frame, a hint of two pine trunks and, far off, one snowy peak, half lost in bare paper. Bare paper at the frame's edges.

No people, no hands, no tools other than the pan.
