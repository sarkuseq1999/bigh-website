A loose sumi-e ink-wash landscape painted on plain warm white paper, wide format (landscape, 3:2), in grey and black ink, with a little gold leaf on one sunlit ridge and nowhere else. The hand of a quick, confident ink-and-mist painting: wet soft edges, ink bleeding into the paper, dry-brush streaks, very few strokes and lots of bare paper. The more it looks like a painting, the better.

The place is California: a dirt trail over rolling golden foothills early on a clear morning. The low morning sun is outside the picture on the right.
