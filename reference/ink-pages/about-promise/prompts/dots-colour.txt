ORDER: four round watercolour dots, one in each of four California colours, in a row from left to right:
1. California poppy orange (a warm, clear orange, not red),
2. golden-hill straw and ochre (a warm dry-grass gold, not bright yellow, not metallic),
3. Pacific blue (a soft sea blue with a touch of green),
4. sage and live-oak green (a soft grey-green).
Each dot is ONE loose, wet bloom of watercolour dropped on the paper with a round brush: roughly round but not a perfect circle, its edge soft and a little ragged where the wet paint bled into the paper, the colour pooling a little darker at one edge and paler in the middle, a little granulation. Flat, no shading into a ball or a sphere, no highlight. All four the same size and the same kind of mark, only the colour changes.
