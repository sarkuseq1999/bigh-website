ORDER: four California native wildflowers, each ONE single flower head seen from straight above, round like a coin, with no stem and no leaves, in a row from left to right:
1. a California poppy, wide open: four broad, silky clear-orange petals, a paler yellow-orange centre,
2. baby blue eyes (Nemophila): five rounded sky-blue petals, paler toward a small white centre,
3. tidy tips (Layia): a ring of golden-yellow ray petals with white tips, a small yellow centre,
4. farewell-to-spring (Clarkia): four soft pink-lilac petals, a little darker toward the middle.
Each flower is painted in a few wet washes, the petals' edges soft and bleeding, no outlines, no veins, no detailed stamens. All four about the same size, each a small round flower on bare paper.
