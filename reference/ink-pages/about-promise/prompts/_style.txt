STYLE: Small paintings for a California brand's website, in loose thin watercolour (a little soft grey ink at most) on plain warm white paper. Use the attached paintings only as the reference for the brush hand and the palette (do not paint what they show): the same painter, the same few wet washes with soft bleeding edges, a little granulation, very few strokes, lots of bare paper. The more it looks like a painting, the better. Not photo-real, not a botanical or detailed drawing, not a cartoon, sticker, icon or children's-book illustration, no pen outlines.

LIGHT AND WARM: these four small paintings replace four grey ink dots that now look dull beside the page's newer soft-colour paintings (a poppy meadow, a sunny oak, a valley at dawn). Keep them light, sunny and friendly, in clear but soft California colour. No black, no heavy dark masses.

LAYOUT: exactly FOUR separate small paintings standing in ONE straight horizontal row across the middle of a wide sheet, evenly spaced, the same size as each other (each about a fifth of the sheet's width), with wide bare paper between them and all round them. They never touch or overlap each other or the edge of the sheet. No ground line, no shadow, no table, no background, no frame, no border: just the four on bare paper.

It is an American, California picture, in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters, NOT an Asian ink painting: no ink-blot sumi look, no scroll, no characters, no seal or stamp.

NEVER: no text, letters, numbers or writing anywhere, no signature, no labels, no lab glassware, no pills, capsules or tablets, no powder, no glass, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo, no sparkle, no gold.
