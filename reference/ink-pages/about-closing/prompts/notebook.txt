ORDER: A naturalist's field notebook open on a sun-warmed granite boulder beside a creek in the California foothills, on a bright spring morning. A wide still-life painting seen from a little above.

On the boulder: the notebook lies open; on its cream pages a loose pencil sketch of a California poppy and an oak leaf, with a few tiny strokes of notes beside them (nothing legible). Laid next to the notebook: a fresh orange California poppy with its feathery leaves, a valley-oak leaf with two acorns, a small brass hand lens, and a pair of round tortoiseshell reading glasses, folded, resting on the page's corner.

Around the boulder: a few tufts of green spring grass and a couple of tiny blue lupines; behind it, the creek sparkles softly over pebbles (pale blue and sand), and beyond, out of focus and pale, a golden hillside with one valley oak. Dappled morning sun; soft warm shadows.

Colours: warm granite grey, cream paper, poppy orange, oak green, lupine blue, pale creek blue, golden straw. The objects are the centre, about two thirds of the painting's width; the whole painting fades into white paper at its edges.
