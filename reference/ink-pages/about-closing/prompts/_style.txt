STYLE: One painting for a California brand's website, in loose ink and thin watercolour on plain warm white paper. Use the attached paintings only as the reference for the brush hand and the palette (do not paint what they show): the same painter, an ink brush drawing first, then a few light wet washes of soft colour with soft bleeding edges, a little granulation, few strokes, lots of bare paper. The more it looks like a painting, the better. Not photo-real, not a detailed drawing, not a cartoon or children's-book illustration, no pen outlines.

LIGHT AND WARM: this painting closes the About page, under a poppy meadow, a sunny valley oak, a valley at dawn and a coloured giant sequoia, all in soft, sunny California colour. Keep it light, sunny and friendly, never saturated. No black masses; the ink is a soft warm grey-brown.

EDGES: the painting is a vignette. Its washes fade softly into the bare white paper on every side, with no hard edge, no frame, no border and no rectangle of background.

It is an American, California picture, in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters, NOT an Asian ink painting: no ink-blot sumi look, no scroll, no characters, no seal or stamp.

PEOPLE (only where the order asks for them): painted loosely in a few strokes and washes, seen from the side or behind; faces are two or three soft strokes at most, never a storybook face; hands loose, no nails, creases or veins.

NEVER: no text, letters, numbers or legible writing anywhere, no signature, no labels, no logos, no lab glassware, no test tubes or beakers, no pills, capsules or tablets, no powder, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo, no sparkle, no gold.
