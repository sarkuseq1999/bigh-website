COMPOSITION: Square, 1024 x 1024. An old wooden carousel on a California seaside pier on a sunny afternoon. The story: a carousel that has turned for about a hundred years and still delights the children who ride it today.

- In the middle, the largest thing in the picture: an old carousel seen from a little to the side, its round tent-like canopy striped in soft cream and pale sky blue, a scalloped edge round its rim. Under it, a ring of carved wooden horses in mid-leap on their poles, painted in pale cream, soft grey and faded sage.
- Two or three small children ride the horses, one waving; a grandparent stands beside one horse, a hand on the child's back. Loose, small figures in a few strokes each.
- GOLD LEAF: only the carved scalloped trim round the canopy's rim is flat matte gold leaf (real old carousels were gilded with gold leaf). This is the only gold in the picture.
- Behind: the pier's wooden railing, the Pacific as a pale blue wash with bare-paper glints, one or two tall palms in loose strokes, a pale sky.
- The pier's boards: warm grey-brown washes, a little shadow under the carousel.

Mood: sunny, happy, nostalgic, light. No signs, no text, no flags, no lights or bulbs, no birds, no red.
