COMPOSITION: Square, 1024 x 1024. A San Francisco morning still life on an old wooden bakery table by an open window. The story: a bakery that still bakes every loaf from a sourdough starter it has kept alive and unchanged for well over a hundred years.

- In front, a little right of centre, the largest thing in the picture: a round loaf of San Francisco sourdough on a worn wooden board, its crust a warm golden-brown and ochre wash, dusted with a little pale flour, scored across the top so the crust has burst open in one curling ridge (the baker's "ear").
- GOLD LEAF: that one burst-open ridge of the crust is flat matte gold leaf. This is the only gold in the picture.
- Beside the board, a bread knife and two cut slices showing the airy, open crumb.
- To the left: an old stoneware crock, pale cream with one soft blue-grey band, a little pale bubbly starter just showing at its rim, a folded linen cloth half over it and a wooden spoon resting across it. Clearly a baker's crock, not a jar, no label.
- The table: warm, worn wood in thin honey and grey-brown washes.
- Through the open window behind: San Francisco in soft morning fog, a hill of pale painted Victorian houses in thin cream, pale sky blue and sage washes, and a glimpse of the bay, mostly bare paper. No bridge.

Mood: warm, homely, a fresh morning. No people, no glass jars, no bottles, no labels, no signs, no text.
