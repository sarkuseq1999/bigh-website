One lone valley oak standing on a bare, gently rounded hill of dry summer grass, painted large, filling the picture. The oak's trunk and spreading branches are bold, confident strokes of dark ink; its rounded crown a few broad wet dabs of grey-green wash with paper showing through. The hill is one long, soft curve of pale warm ochre wash under it. The sky above is clear, empty paper, with one small round sun of real gold leaf high to the right of the crown, crisp and bright, nothing else in the sky. Calm, open, warm.
