A quiet Pacific beach at dawn, painted loose and spare, filling the picture. The lower half is wet sand: one broad, smooth, pale warm-grey wash, shining and flat, lightest in the exact middle where an object will stand, with the faintest reflection of the sky in it. Across the middle of the picture, one long, soft line of foam where a small spent wave has just reached the sand: a single dry-brush sweep of white and pale grey. Above it, calm sea in two or three long horizontal strokes of pale blue-grey wash, and above that a soft band of morning mist fading up into empty paper. No gold. Nothing else: no rocks, no shells, no birds, no people, no boats, no sun. Wide, still, calm.
