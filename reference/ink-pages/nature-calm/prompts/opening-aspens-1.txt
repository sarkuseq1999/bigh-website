STYLE: An ink-and-wash painting on plain white paper, in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters. The land is first drawn and shaded with a brush in grey and black ink, quickly and loosely, with dry-brush strokes; then a few light, transparent watercolour washes are laid over it. The sky is one big wet wash with no ink in it. The ink does the work on the land; the colour is light and calm. Lots of bare paper. You can see every brush stroke; soft bleeding edges where the paper was wet, crisp ones where it was dry. Richness comes from brushwork and ink, never from fine or photographic detail. The more it looks like a painting by a grown-up painter, the better. Not photo-real, no smooth digital gradients, no pen outlines, not a storybook or greeting-card illustration, not a cartoon. An American, California scene, NOT an Asian ink painting: no misty peaks, no bands of fog, no pine tree clinging to a rock, no cranes, no boats.

THE PICTURE: the opening painting of a product page: the Eastern Sierra in early October on a still afternoon: a grove of quaking aspens turned gold, white trunks, granite peaks pale and far behind, and almost all of it is SKY. Large dark lettering and a product will be laid over it later, so the painting must stay simple, calm and light. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

COMPOSITION, top to bottom (keep these heights):
- From the top edge down to 60% of the height: open sky. One big wet wash of deep clear high-mountain blue, mid-pale at the top, paler toward the peaks, uneven as a real wash is, with granulation, brush direction showing, and one or two soft backruns. Two small soft clouds left as bare white paper, high up near the top corners only.
- From 56% to 64%: far granite peaks across the whole width in very pale grey-violet washes with a touch of early snow left as bare paper, their tops never above 56% of the height. Calm and pale.
- From 62% to 75%: two groves of quaking aspens in the foreground, one from the left edge to 38% across and one from 62% across to the right edge: slender white trunks drawn with a few dark ink marks and eyes, their crowns loose dabs and washes of gold, butter yellow and a little orange, low (their crowns no higher than 62% of the height); a few gold leaves fallen on the grass at their feet. In the very middle of the picture (from 42% to 58% across) the ground is flat, low and open, short dry grass only: an EMPTY spot where an object will be placed later. Nothing stands there.
- From 75% to 80%: the grass thins out downward into a few loose dry-brush strokes and specks.
- The bottom 20% of the picture: completely bare white paper. Nothing there.

EDGES: the sky, the land and the water run off the left and right edges, and the sky off the top edge, with no fade, no vignette and no border. The bottom ends in bare white paper, never in a straight painted line.

NEVER: no text, letters or numbers anywhere, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold paint or gold leaf, no bottles, jars, glasses or products, no pills, capsules or powder, no people, no animals, no birds, no boats, no buildings, no road, no tents, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo, no sun disc, no moon, no stars.
