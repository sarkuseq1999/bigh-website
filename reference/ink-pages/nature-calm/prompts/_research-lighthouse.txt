A small white lighthouse on a rocky Pacific headland in daylight, filling the picture. The tower is tall and simple: its white sides are bare paper edged with a few pale grey strokes, a dark lantern room and railing at the top in a few confident black touches, a small keeper's house beside it in two or three grey strokes. The headland is bold, blotted strokes of dark ink falling away to the sea on the right; the sea is a calm, pale, even grey wash to the horizon, with one soft line of foam where it meets the rock. The sky is empty paper. No gold. Nothing else: no boats, no birds, no people, no trees. Steady, clear, calm.
