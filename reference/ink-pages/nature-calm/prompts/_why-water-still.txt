The surface of a still mountain lake at dusk, seen from its shore, filling the picture. The far shore is one long, soft, horizontal grey stroke a third of the way down, with a low rounded hill above it in the palest wash; everything above is empty paper. The water below is perfectly still: a smooth, pale, even grey wash, mirror-flat, with no ripples at all. Down the middle of the water, from the far shore to the bottom edge, runs one straight, narrow, unbroken column of real gold leaf: the reflection of a low sun or moon that is not itself in the picture, bright and crisp against the grey. In the lower left, one smooth dark stone breaks the surface, painted in two or three bold wet strokes, its soft reflection under it. Nothing else: no trees, no boats, no birds, no reeds, no people. Deep quiet.
