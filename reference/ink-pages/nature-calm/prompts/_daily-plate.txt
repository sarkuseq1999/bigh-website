The end of a simple meal, seen from a little above, filling the picture: one round plate, empty except for a few faint grey smears, painted as a single pale grey ring with a darker rim; a fork laid across its right edge in two confident dark strokes; a folded cloth napkin in soft grey wash to the left. In front of the plate, in a short row, three dietary-supplement capsules: each a small, smooth, elongated oblong pill made of two rounded halves that meet in the middle, like a vitamin capsule, painted pale cream-white with a fine grey outline and a small soft shadow; never a coffee pod, never a tablet. No gold. Nothing else: no food, no glass, no table edge, no hands. Quiet, finished, calm.
