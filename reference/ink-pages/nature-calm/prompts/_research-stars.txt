The seven stars of the Big Dipper at dusk, large, filling the upper two thirds of the picture: seven small, separate points of real gold leaf, each a crisp little round fleck, set exactly in the Big Dipper's shape (four stars making the bowl, three the curved handle), bright against the pale paper. The sky is almost bare paper, with only the faintest cool grey wash deepening a little toward the top. Along the bottom of the picture, one long, soft, dark ridge line: a single confident sweep of wet black-grey ink, a low hill's silhouette fading into the paper at both ends. Nothing else: no trees, no moon, no clouds, no other stars, no buildings, no people. Clear, quiet, steady.
