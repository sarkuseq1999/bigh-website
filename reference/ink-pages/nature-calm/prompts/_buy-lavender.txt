A few rows of lavender in a California farm field, painted loose and airy in watercolour and ink, filling the lower two thirds of the picture, with the upper third empty paper. Each row is a low mound of soft grey-green stalks in quick upward strokes, topped with small spikes of lavender flowers dabbed in pale violet and dusty purple wash, paper showing through everywhere; the nearest row is boldest at the bottom edge, the rows behind it paler and simpler, fading into the paper. In the exact middle of the picture, a clear empty space between the stalks the width of a wine bottle, from the ground wash to the top of the rows, where nothing is painted (an object will stand there). The ground under the nearest row is one soft, warm, pale grey wash. No gold. Nothing else: no buildings, no sky colour, no sun, no people, no bees, no fences. Soft, fragrant, calm.
