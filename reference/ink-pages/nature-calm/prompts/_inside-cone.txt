One single long sugar pine cone from the Sierra Nevada, lying diagonally across the picture and filling it, seen from the side. Its many overlapping scales are painted in quick, confident dry-brush strokes of warm brown and grey ink, each scale one or two touches, darker where they overlap, with paper showing through between them; the cone is loose and expressive, never a detailed botanical drawing. A short piece of stem at its top end. On three or four scale tips, a tiny drop of clear resin painted as a small bead of real gold leaf. A faint soft grey shadow under the cone. Nothing else: no branch, no needles, no tree, no ground. Calm, natural, complete.
