THE PICTURE: a painting for a website's Science page, beside the words "You are made of about 37 trillion cells, and each one makes its own energy." Later, every window in it will light up one by one, like a town at dusk: each house keeping its own lamp lit, as each cell makes its own energy. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

THE SCENE: San Francisco at early dusk, seen from a little across a valley: rows of narrow Victorian houses with bay windows and little gables step up a steep hill, side by side, one row above another; more houses cover the rounded hills behind; at the very back, the bay and the East Bay hills as one pale blue-grey wash. A few dark cypress or street trees between the houses. The houses are drawn in ink with pale washes (cream, pale blue-grey, sage, a soft butter yellow), each house's bay window and its other windows clearly drawn as small rectangles.

THE WINDOWS: there are many windows, about 80 to 120 in all, small, clearly separated, spread over the whole town (near houses have bigger windows, far ones tiny). Every window is still UNLIT: bare white paper inside each window frame, so each pane reads as an empty white rectangle. No light anywhere yet, no yellow, no glow.

COMPOSITION:
- The town fills the middle of the picture from about 22% to 88% down, across the full width, running off the left and right edges with no fade at the sides.
- Above 22% down: open dusk sky, bare paper with only a very pale peach and lavender wash low near the hilltops. No sun, no moon, no stars, no birds.
- Below 88% down: bare paper; the nearest houses' foundations and the street fade out softly; no hard edge.
- No people, no cars, no cable cars, no bridges, no signs.
