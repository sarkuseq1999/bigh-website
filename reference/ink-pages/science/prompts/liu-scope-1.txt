THE PICTURE: a painting for a website's Science page, for the part that introduces the company's chief scientist, Dr. Liu, who has studied mitochondria for decades. It shows him at work: he has just looked up from his microscope, as if we walked into his study. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

THE ATTACHED PICTURES:
1. A photograph of Dr. Liu. Paint THIS man: his face, his age, his thin metal-rimmed glasses, his dark hair with its side parting, his warm half-smile. He must be recognisable as the same person.
2. A painted portrait of the same man: it shows how he should look when painted (the loose ink strokes of his dark jacket, the soft watercolour of his face).
3. A painting of the Big Sur coast: take only its way of painting (see STYLE), not its subject.

THE SCENE:
- He sits at an old wooden desk on the right side of the picture, seen from the front, from the waist up, his body turned a little toward the left. His head is turned a little to his right (our left), exactly as in the photograph, and he looks straight at us with the same warm half-smile. His jacket is off: a pale blue shirt with the sleeves rolled to the forearm, and his blue striped tie.
- In front of him on the desk, at about 52% across, stands an old brass microscope (a plain upright one, a few loose strokes of ochre and brown, no fine detail). His right hand rests on its focus knob; his left forearm rests on the desk.
- On the desk beside it: an open notebook with a few soft grey lines (no letters), and a pencil. Nothing else.
- Behind him, at the far right, a tall window: pale morning light, and through it, barely there, a soft golden California hill and one eucalyptus. Light falls from the window across the desk.
- The desk runs out of the picture on the right, and on the left it fades into bare paper at about 36% across.

THE GOLD: for this order only, the rule "no gold paint" is lifted for ONE thing: on the microscope's stage, where the sample lies, one small fleck of flat, matte, warm gold, like a scrap of gold leaf. No glow, no shine, no sparkle, no halo. No gold anywhere else.

COMPOSITION:
- From the left edge to 36% across: bare paper, completely empty, from top to bottom (large dark words will sit there).
- His head sits at about 72% across and 30% down. Nothing dark or busy behind his head: plain pale wall there.
- The scene fades into bare paper at the bottom edge; no floor.

NEVER: no lab coat, no lab glassware of any kind (no flasks, beakers, test tubes, jars, bottles, dishes), no computer, no screen, no second person, no writing, letters or numbers anywhere. Not a photograph, not a smooth digital portrait: the same loose ink and watercolour as the rest, his face a little more careful than the rest so that he is recognisable.

Paint a NEW picture.
