THE PICTURE: an idea for the opening painting of a website's Science page. It fills the whole first screen under the large words "Our science starts in the cell." The viewer is INSIDE one living cell, as if floating in it: a wide, quiet, light-filled place, like looking through clear shallow water over pale sand on a sunny morning. Earlier takes were lovely but their washes had scalloped, cloud-like edges and read as mist over a dark hill, like an East Asian screen painting. This take must read as clear water and light, never as mist, cloud, mountain or rock. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

THE SCENE:
- Soft wet washes fill the whole picture from edge to edge: very pale blue-grey running into sage and a little warm sand colour, laid broadly wet-in-wet so that they melt into each other. NO scalloped or cauliflower cloud edges, no rows of cloud shapes, no bands of mist. A few large, soft, lighter patches, like sunlight moving on the bottom of a clear pool.
- The nucleus: ONE large deep blue-grey wash at about 72% across and 60% down, about 40% of the picture's height: a soft round pool of deep colour, wet-in-wet, darkest at its heart and melting outward, with NO hard outline and no dry-brush rocky edge. It must not read as a boulder, a hill or a moon: it is a deep place in the water.
- The mitochondria: about forty SLENDER gold marks drifting through the whole space like willow leaves on still water. Each is one single short brush stroke of flat, matte, warm gold, like a sliver of gold leaf laid on the paper: long and thin (about five times longer than wide), gently curved, of different lengths, pointing every which way. Depth: a few big ones near the bottom and right edges (as long as a tenth of the picture's width), most of middle size, and small pale ones far off. Several gather loosely round the nucleus, some lying across its edge. No ink outline round them, no lines inside them. They are the only strong colour and the first thing the eye finds.
- Two very pale, thin, curved lines sweeping round the nucleus like ripples, barely there.

THE GOLD: for this order only, the rule "no gold paint" is lifted for these marks and nowhere else. Flat and matte, like gold leaf: no glow, no shine, no sparkle, no halo, no yellow light round them.

NEVER: no mist, fog, clouds, mountains, hills, rocks or waves; no bean, peanut, sausage or capsule shapes; no neat even spacing; no outlines round the gold; nothing that could read as germs or worms: no heads, tails, hairs, spikes or segments, no small dots in clusters, no red, pink, purple or bright green. No bubbles drawn as rings. No fish, no plants, no people. No text, labels, pointers, scale bar, microscope, lens or ring.

COMPOSITION:
- The upper left (from the left edge to about 55% across, from the top to about 50% down) stays very pale, nearly bare paper, with at most three small far-off gold slivers, so that large dark words can sit there.
- The washes run off all four edges: no outline of the whole cell, no white border, no vignette, no fade to white at the edges.
