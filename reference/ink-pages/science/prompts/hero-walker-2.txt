THE PICTURE: an idea for the opening painting of a website's Science page. It fills the whole first screen under the large words "Our science starts in the cell." ONE walker is the clear main subject, large and near the middle, coming toward us. This walker is painted as if we could see the energy inside: the body is one pale, translucent wet wash, and slender slivers of gold leaf drift inside it. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

THE SCENE: late morning on rolling golden California hills: dry grass in light gold washes, two or three dark valley oaks far back, a pale blue-grey coast range on the horizon. A dirt trail comes down toward the bottom edge. On it, ONE walker in mid-stride comes toward us, seen from the front and a little to one side: the plain shape of a person walking, with a sun hat and a walking stick, relaxed and upright. The walker is about 54% of the picture's height and stands at about 64% across, the feet at about 90% down.

HOW THE WALKER IS PAINTED: not clothed in detail. The whole body is ONE soft, pale wash (blue-grey running into sage and a little warm grey, like clear water), its edge simply where the wet wash stopped, with a broken ink brush line along the shaded side only. The hat and the stick are a few plain ink strokes. No face.
Inside the body, about forty-five SLENDER gold marks: each one single short brush stroke of flat, matte, warm gold, like a sliver of gold leaf, long and thin (about five times longer than wide), gently curved, pointing every which way. They are everywhere in the body but gather most thickly in three places: the head, the heart, and the thighs and calves. They are the only strong colour and the first thing the eye finds.

THE GOLD: for this order only, the rule "no gold paint" is lifted for these marks and nowhere else. Flat and matte, like gold leaf: no glow, no shine, no sparkle, no halo, no yellow light round the figure. None outside the body.

COMPOSITION:
- The upper left (from the left edge to about 52% across, from the top to about 55% down) is open sky: bare paper with only the palest warm wash, so that large dark words can sit there. No clouds with hard edges, no sun, no birds.
- The hills run off the left, right and bottom edges with no fade and no vignette. A few California poppies low in the grass near the bottom left.
- Only one person. No dog, no buildings, no fences, no signs.

NEVER: not a ghost, a spirit, a robot, an X-ray or an anatomy chart: no bones, no organs, no veins, no skull. No dots in clusters; no red or rust; no bean or capsule shapes. No text, no labels, no pointers.

Paint a NEW picture; the attached painting gives only the way of painting. Paint it loosely: quick ink brush strokes, wet washes, lots of bare paper.
