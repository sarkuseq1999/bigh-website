THE SCENE: a bright afternoon on rolling golden California hills: dry grass, a valley oak far off on a ridge, a pale blue-grey coast range on the horizon, a narrow dirt trail. On a flat grassy spot beside the trail, the grandfather swings the child round and round by both hands: the "airplane" spin every grandparent knows. He stands with his feet planted wide, leaning back a little, seen in three-quarter view from the front left, laughing. The child flies out level beside him, held by both hands, legs streaming out behind, sun hat tumbling off into the air, laughing. Their joined hands (his two big hands round the child's two small ones) are plain to see at the middle of the picture, between them. Dry grass and a little dust kicked up round his feet; the child's t-shirt and his shirt flutter with the speed. It reads at once as pure joy and energy.

THE FACES: painted in a few loose brush strokes only, seen from the side: closed, happy eyes as small curved strokes, an open laughing mouth. No detailed features, no realistic portrait.

- The upper left (from the left edge to about 52% across, from the top to about 48% down) is open sky: bare paper with only the palest warm wash, so that large dark words can sit there. No clouds with hard edges, no sun, no birds.
- The hills run off the left, right and bottom edges with no fade and no vignette; the land fills the bottom 46%.
