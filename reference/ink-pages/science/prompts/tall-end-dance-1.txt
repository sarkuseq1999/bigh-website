TWO PAINTINGS ARE ATTACHED. The first (an older couple swing-dancing on a sunny path in Golden Gate Park, she spinning under his raised hand in a blue dress, a small band and other dancers behind) is the picture to paint again: the same couple, the same clothes and colours, the same place, light and loose hand. The second (the Big Sur coast) gives only the way of painting.

THE PICTURE: the first painting again as a TALL picture for a phone's screen, where the words "Why start with the cell? Everything you do runs on energy your cells make." stand in its sky. Portrait, 1024 x 1536.

SIZE AND PLACE (important):
- The top 42% of the picture is open air: bare paper with only the palest warm wash, so that large dark words can sit there. Tree branches may come in only along the right edge (right of 80% across) in that top part.
- The couple stands in the middle: together they fill the width from about 24% to 78% across. He is about 34% of the picture's height, the top of his cap at about 54% down, his feet at about 88% down. Their joined hands, raised, at about 50% across, 47% down. Nothing that matters is in the outer 18% at the left or the right.
- The band and other dancers small and pale behind them, between 60% and 75% down.

Faces in two or three loose strokes only, not storybook faces. Paint a NEW picture. Loose, quick ink brush strokes, light washes, lots of bare paper.
