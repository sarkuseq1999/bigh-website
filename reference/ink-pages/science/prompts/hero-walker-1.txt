THE PICTURE: an idea for the opening painting of a website's Science page. It fills the whole first screen under the large words "Our science starts in the cell." ONE walker is the clear main subject, large and near the middle, coming toward us; small slivers of gold leaf lie on the painting where the body makes the most energy. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

THE SCENE: late morning on rolling golden California hills: dry grass, two or three dark valley oaks far back, a pale blue-grey coast range on the horizon. A dirt trail comes down the middle distance toward the bottom edge. On it, ONE older walker (about sixty-five, fit, unhurried) walks toward us in mid-stride, seen from the front and a little to one side: a sun hat, a light shirt with the sleeves rolled, the straps of a small daypack, plain trousers, one hand on a wooden walking stick, the other arm swinging free. The face is in the shade of the hat brim: two or three soft strokes, calm, no detailed features, a figure and not a portrait. The walker is about 52% of the picture's height and stands at about 64% across, the feet at about 90% down. Painted in sure ink strokes and a few light washes, with bare paper for the light on the shirt.

THE GOLD: for this order only, the rule "no gold paint" is lifted for these marks and nowhere else. About thirty SLENDER gold marks lie on the walker's body, as if gold leaf had been laid on the finished painting: each one single short brush stroke of flat, matte, warm gold, long and thin (about five times longer than wide), gently curved, of different lengths and directions. They gather in three places only: a few at the head (the brow under the hat), a small loose group over the heart, and more along both thighs and calves. None on the hat, the pack, the stick, the grass or the sky. Flat and matte: no glow, no shine, no sparkle, no halo, no yellow light round them.

COMPOSITION:
- The upper left (from the left edge to about 52% across, from the top to about 55% down) is open sky: bare paper with only the palest warm wash, so that large dark words can sit there. No clouds with hard edges, no sun, no birds.
- The hills run off the left, right and bottom edges with no fade and no vignette. A few California poppies low in the grass near the bottom left.
- Only one person. No dog, no buildings, no fences, no signs.

NEVER: the gold must not read as armour, patches, bandages, medals or jewellery: thin loose slivers only, never solid plates; no dots in clusters; no red or rust. No text, no labels, no pointers.

Paint a NEW picture; the attached painting gives only the way of painting. Paint it loosely: quick ink brush strokes, light washes, lots of bare paper.
