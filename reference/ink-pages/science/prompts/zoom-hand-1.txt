THE PICTURE: the second view of a slow zoom on a website's Science page. The first view was a walker on a trail in golden California hills; this one has moved in close on the walker's hand; the next will be one cell of that hand. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

THE SCENE: seen close, from the side: an older walker's hand and forearm, sleeve rolled to the elbow, hanging relaxed at their side in mid-stride, the fingertips just brushing the tops of tall dry golden grass as they walk past. One or two California poppies among the grass. Behind, far out of focus, the golden hill and pale sky as two or three soft washes. The hand is large: about 45% of the picture's height, a little right of the middle. It is drawn with a few sure ink brush strokes and a light warm wash: a natural, kind, slightly weathered hand with five fingers, correct and calm; no rings, no watch, no fine wrinkles, no photographic detail.

COMPOSITION:
- Only the hand, the forearm, the edge of the shirt at the elbow and a little of the trouser leg and hip behind it: no face, no torso beyond that.
- Open bare paper in the upper left third.
- The grass and washes run off the left, right and bottom edges with no fade and no vignette.
