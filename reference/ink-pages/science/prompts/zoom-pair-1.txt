THE PICTURE: the last view of a slow zoom on a website's Science page. The zoom starts inside one living cell, pulls back to a hand, then to this picture, where the words "Why start with the cell? Everything you do runs on energy your cells make." stand in the sky. The picture it replaces (one small walker on empty hills) felt plain. This one must feel warm and alive at first glance: a grandparent and a grandchild, hand in hand. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

THE SCENE: late morning on rolling golden California hills: dry grass, a valley oak far off, a pale blue-grey coast range on the horizon, a few California poppies by the trail. On a narrow dirt trail, seen from behind and a little to one side, a grandfather and a small child of about five walk away from us hand in hand, in mid-stride. He wears a sun hat and a light shirt with the sleeves rolled; the child wears a small sun hat and looks up at him, one arm lifted, pointing ahead up the trail. Their joined hands are plain to see between them, at the middle of the pair. They are unhurried and happy; it reads at once as a grandparent out walking with a grandchild. Painted in sure ink strokes and a few light washes: figures, not portraits, no faces drawn.

SIZE AND PLACE (important): the grandfather is about 42% of the picture's height; the top of his hat is at about 46% down from the top edge, the feet at about 88% down. The pair stand at about 62% across. Everything above 40% down, across the whole width, is open sky or far hills.

COMPOSITION:
- The upper left (from the left edge to about 55% across, from the top to about 50% down) is open sky: bare paper with only the palest warm wash, so that large dark words can sit there. No clouds with hard edges, no sun, no birds.
- The hills run off the left, right and bottom edges with no fade and no vignette; the land fills the bottom 48%.
- Only these two people. No dog, no buildings, no fences, no signs.
- A little more colour than a plain landscape: the gold of the grass a touch richer near the pair, the poppies a clear orange, the child's shirt one small note of soft blue.
