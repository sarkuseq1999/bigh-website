THE PICTURE: the last view of a slow zoom on a website's Science page. The zoom starts inside one living cell, pulls back to a hand, then to this picture, where the words "Why start with the cell? Everything you do runs on energy your cells make." stand in the sky. The picture it replaces (one small walker on empty hills) felt plain and pale. This one must have more colour and life: someone picking oranges in a California orange grove. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

THE SCENE: a sunny morning in an old California orange grove. A woman in her sixties, fit and at ease, stands under an orange tree at the right and reaches up with one hand to pick an orange from a low branch; her other arm holds a shallow basket with a few oranges in it. She wears a straw sun hat and a light shirt with the sleeves rolled. She is seen from the side and a little behind (no face drawn: the line of the cheek under the hat brim only). The orange tree comes in from the right edge: a dark, loosely brushed sage-green crown with seven or eight bright oranges in it, each one a single round dab of clear orange, well apart from each other. Behind her, rows of orange trees fall away down a gentle slope into a pale valley, with a blue-grey mountain range far off. Dry golden grass and a few California poppies at her feet. Painted in sure ink strokes and light washes: a figure, not a portrait.

THE HAND: the hand that reaches for the orange is plain to see against the sky or the pale far hills, fingers just closing round the fruit. It is painted simply, in a few strokes.

SIZE AND PLACE (important): the woman is about 46% of the picture's height; the top of her hat is at about 44% down from the top edge, her feet at about 90% down. She stands at about 62% across. Her raised hand and the branch are right of 60% across. The tree's crown stays right of 66% across and may reach the top edge there.

COMPOSITION:
- The upper left (from the left edge to about 55% across, from the top to about 50% down) is open sky: bare paper with only the palest warm wash, so that large dark words can sit there. No clouds with hard edges, no sun, no birds.
- The land runs off the left, right and bottom edges with no fade and no vignette.
- Only one person. No ladder, no crates with writing, no buildings, no fences, no signs.
- The oranges are the brightest colour in the picture. No red, no rust, no small dots in clusters.
