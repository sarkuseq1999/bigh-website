THE PICTURE: an idea for the opening painting of a website's Science page. It fills the whole first screen under the large words "Our science starts in the cell." The viewer is INSIDE one living cell, as if floating in it: a wide, quiet, slightly mysterious place, like drifting in clear shallow water at first light. It must be a real painting someone would frame, wet and loose, never a schoolbook diagram. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

THE SCENE:
- Soft wet washes fill the whole picture from edge to edge: very pale blue-grey running into sage and a little warm grey, with blooms and back-runs where the paper was wet. Palest (almost bare paper) in the upper left, deeper toward the right and the bottom.
- The nucleus: ONE large deep blue-grey ink-and-wash form at about 72% across and 60% down, about 40% of the picture's height, wet-in-wet, darker on one side, its edge bleeding softly into the wash round it. It sits there like a low moon or a great smooth boulder under water.
- The mitochondria: about forty SLENDER gold marks drifting through the whole space like willow leaves on still water. Each is one single short brush stroke of flat, matte, warm gold, like a sliver of gold leaf laid on the paper: long and thin (about five times longer than wide), gently curved, of different lengths, pointing every which way. Depth: a few big ones near the bottom and right edges (as long as a tenth of the picture's width), most of middle size, and small pale ones far off. Several gather loosely round the nucleus. No ink outline round them, no lines inside them. They are the only strong colour and the first thing the eye finds.
- Two or three very pale, broad, curved grey ink strokes sweeping round the nucleus, barely there. Far off along the top right, one faint pale arc: the inside of the cell's own edge, seen from within.

THE GOLD: for this order only, the rule "no gold paint" is lifted for these marks and nowhere else. Flat and matte, like gold leaf: no glow, no shine, no sparkle, no halo, no yellow light round them.

NEVER: no bean, peanut, sausage or capsule shapes; no neat even spacing; no outlines round the gold; nothing that could read as germs or worms: no heads, tails, hairs, spikes or segments, no small dots in clusters, no red, pink, purple or bright green. No bubbles drawn as rings. No fish, no plants, no people. No text, labels, pointers, scale bar, microscope, lens or ring.

COMPOSITION:
- The upper left (from the left edge to about 55% across, from the top to about 50% down) stays very pale, nearly bare paper, with at most three small far-off gold slivers, so that large dark words can sit there.
- The washes run off all four edges: no outline of the whole cell, no white border, no vignette, no fade to white at the edges.
