THE PICTURE: the first attached picture is an ink painting on a website's Science page. Make a NEW version of it, the same size and shape as it (landscape), with exactly the same drawing: the same place, the same composition, every building, tree and hill in the same spot and the same size, the same brush strokes, the same bare paper.

THE CHANGE: lay a few light, transparent watercolour washes over the ink, as the painter of the second attached picture (the Big Sur coast) does: dry-grass gold and sage on the hills and grass, sage and deep sage on the trees, a soft blue-grey on anything far away and on water, a pale warm cream on sunlit walls and stone. The ink still does the work; the colour is light and calm, and lots of the paper stays bare white. Keep it a California plein-air watercolour in spirit.

NEVER: do not add or remove anything; no sky colour, no clouds, no sun disc, no gold, no text, no frame, no border, no glow.
