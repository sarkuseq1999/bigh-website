THE IDEA: the rower at sunrise, seen CLOSE. A still California lake at first light, low golden hills dotted with dark valley oaks along the far shore. A fit man in his late sixties rows a long, narrow racing single scull, seen from the side and quite near: he fills a good part of the picture. He is mid-stroke, leaning back, arms drawn in to his chest, both long oars sweeping back, the blade on our side just lifting out of the water and dripping. He wears a pale cap, a light coral top and dark shorts; his face in two or three loose strokes. Around the blade, and trailing behind the boat to the left, the round ripples his blades left at each stroke, growing wider and fainter the farther back they are. Calm, rhythm, quiet strength. Clear air, no fog, no mist.

THE GOLD (important): the low sun (out of the picture, to the right) lights the ripples. Paint ONLY the ripple rings round the blades and behind the boat as thin strokes of flat, even, pale warm ochre-yellow (they will be laid in gold leaf later); everything else in normal colour. No other yellow on the water.

SIZE AND PLACE: the rower is about 34% of the picture's height: his cap at about 40% down, the hull at about 74% down. He sits at about 70% across; the boat runs from about 44% across off the right edge. The near oar reaches down to the water at about 58% across, 80% down. The ripple rings trail away to the left behind the boat, from about 56% to 10% across, 72% to 82% down. The far shore with its golden hills and oaks lies along about 46% to 54% down.

COLOUR: soft blue-grey water with warm reflections, golden hills, dark sage oaks, the rower's top a pale coral; the ochre ripples the brightest warm note.

Paint a NEW picture; the attached painting gives only the way of painting. Loose, quick ink brush strokes, light washes, lots of bare paper. Not an Asian ink painting: no misty mountains, no fisherman's boat, no reeds in mist; a modern racing shell with long oars, on an American lake.
