This one shows a clear mind and a steady hand at work: a painter at her easel on a California hillside, in the tradition of the California plein-air watercolour painters. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

THE SCENE: late morning on a golden California hillside above the Pacific: dry grass, a valley oak, a pale blue-grey coast range and a thin line of sea far off. An older woman stands at a small wooden folding easel, painting the view. She is seen from behind and well to one side (a three-quarter back view: the line of the cheek and the hat brim show, no face). She wears a wide sun hat and a loose light shirt with the sleeves rolled. Her raised hand holds a brush, its tip just touching the sheet on the easel; her other hand hangs relaxed at her side. On the sheet: a few loose washes, the same hills begun in gold and blue-grey, very simple. A small wooden paint box lies open on the easel's shelf. A canvas bag and a folded jacket in the grass by the easel's legs; a few California poppies near the bottom left.

THE HAND: the hand with the brush is plain to see against the pale sheet, painted simply in a few strokes.
