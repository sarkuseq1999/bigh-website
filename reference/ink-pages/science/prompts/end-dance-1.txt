THE IDEA: Sunday swing dancing in Golden Gate Park, San Francisco. A sunny Sunday afternoon on a paved path under tall Monterey cypresses and eucalyptus. An older couple in their seventies swing-dance: he raises his hand and she spins under it, her full skirt flaring out, both laughing, his other hand open toward her. He wears a light shirt, braces and a flat cap; she wears a cornflower-blue dress and white shoes. Their two joined hands, held high above her head, are plain to see. Farther back, loosely, a few other couples dance and a small jazz band plays (a double bass, a trumpet) under the trees, all blurred and pale. Dappled sunlight on the path. Joy, rhythm, togetherness.

SIZE AND PLACE: the couple is at about 58% to 80% across; he is about 44% of the picture's height, the top of his cap at about 42% down, his feet at about 86% down. Their joined hands, raised, at about 68% across, 36% down. The other dancers and the band are small and pale, at about 30% to 55% across and 84% to 95% across, 50% to 75% down. The trees rise along the right edge and in the far background, never into the upper-left open air.

COLOUR: warm light on the path, sage and blue-green trees, her dress the one strong blue, his shirt pale; the background people only a few pale washes.

Paint a NEW picture; the attached painting gives only the way of painting. Loose, quick ink brush strokes, light washes, lots of bare paper.
