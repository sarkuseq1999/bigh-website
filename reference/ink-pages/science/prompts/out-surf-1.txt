Everything you do runs on energy your cells make." stand in the sky. The picture it replaces (one small walker on empty hills) felt plain and could have been anywhere. This one must say California and energy at first glance: an older surfer walking down to the Pacific at first light. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

THE SCENE: early morning on a wide California beach under golden sandstone bluffs. A fit, silver-haired surfer of about sixty-five walks away from us across the wet sand toward the sea, seen from behind and a little to one side, in mid-stride: a dark spring wetsuit, a long surfboard under one arm, the free hand swinging at the side. Long, low lines of small clean waves roll in, painted in a few blue-grey and sea-green washes with bare paper for the foam. Footprints behind the surfer in the wet sand, which mirrors a little of the sky. The bluffs at the right carry dry golden grass and a few California poppies along their top; a line of far headlands fades down the coast. Painted in sure ink strokes and light washes: a figure, not a portrait, no face.

THE HAND: the free hand, swinging at the surfer's side, is plain to see against the pale wet sand, painted simply in a few strokes.
