TWO PAINTINGS ARE ATTACHED. The first (a grandfather and a small child on a windy golden California hillside, hand in hand, flying a kite) is the picture to paint again: the same two people, the same clothes and colours, the same place, light and wind, the same loose hand. The second (the Big Sur coast) gives only the way of painting.

THE PICTURE: the first painting again as a TALL picture for a phone's screen, where the words "Why start with the cell? Everything you do runs on energy your cells make." stand in its sky. Portrait, 1024 x 1536.

THE SCENE: as in the first painting. A breezy afternoon on rolling golden California hills, the dry grass streaming in the wind, a pale blue-grey coast range on the horizon. On a dirt trail on a crest, seen from behind and a little to one side, the grandfather (pale straw sun hat, pale shirt with the sleeves rolled, khaki trousers) and the small child (little pale sun hat, soft blue t-shirt, tan shorts) stand hand in hand. Their joined hands are plain to see between them, low. The grandfather's free arm is raised, pointing up and to the right. The child's free arm is raised up and to the right as if holding a kite string, the hand closed. NO KITE AND NO STRING in this picture: they are added later. Figures, not portraits: no faces drawn.

SIZE AND PLACE (important):
- The top 44% of the picture is open sky: bare paper with only the palest warm wash, nothing in it at all (no clouds, no sun, no birds, no kite, no lines), so that large dark words can sit there.
- The far range lies along the horizon at about 60% down.
- The grandfather is about 32% of the picture's height: the top of his hat at about 57% down, his feet at about 89% down. He stands at about 40% across; the child at his right at about 56% across. Nothing that matters is in the outer 15% at the left or the right.
- The sky to the right of the grandfather's head, from about 62% to 84% across and 44% to 58% down, stays bare paper (a kite will fly there).

COMPOSITION:
- The trail comes up from the bottom edge to the pair's feet.
- The grass in the foreground streams in the wind, loose dry strokes.
- The land runs off the left, right and bottom edges with no fade and no vignette.
- Only these two people. No dog, no buildings, no fences, no signs, no kite, no string.

Paint a NEW picture. Loose, quick ink brush strokes, light washes, lots of bare paper.
