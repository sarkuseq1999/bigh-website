THE SCENE: a bright, breezy afternoon on rolling golden California hills: dry grass bending in the wind, a valley oak far off on a ridge, a pale blue-grey coast range on the horizon. On a grassy crest by a narrow dirt trail, the grandfather and the child stand hand in hand, flying a kite. They are seen from behind and a little to one side. The child's head is tipped back, looking up at the kite; the child's free hand holds the kite string. The grandfather's free arm is raised, pointing up at the kite. Their joined hands are plain to see between them, low, at the middle of the pair. The wind is in everything: his shirt pressed against his back, the child's t-shirt fluttering, the grass streaming the same way.

THE KITE (important): a simple diamond kite flies high in the upper right of the sky. Paint the kite as ONE flat, even wash of pale warm ochre-yellow inside a crisp dark ink edge, with one thin ink cross-stick: no pattern, no shading, no other colour on it (it will be laid in gold leaf later). From its lower point hangs a long tail: a thin ink line that curls in the wind, with four small bows of the same ochre along it. One single thin dark ink line (the string) runs from the child's raised hand straight up to the kite's middle, sagging only a little. The string and the kite are separate from everything else: nothing overlaps the kite.

SIZE AND PLACE (important): the grandfather is about 40% of the picture's height; the top of his hat at about 48% down from the top edge, his feet at about 88% down. He stands at about 60% across, the child at his right at about 68% across. Their joined hands at about 64% across, 68% down. The kite flies at about 85% across and 17% down; it is about 8% of the picture's width; its tail hangs and curls down to about 36% down. Nothing of the kite or its tail is left of 76% across.

COMPOSITION:
- The upper left (from the left edge to about 58% across, from the top to about 50% down) is open sky: bare paper with only the palest warm wash, so that large dark words can sit there. No clouds with hard edges, no sun, no birds.
- The hills run off the left, right and bottom edges with no fade and no vignette; the land fills the bottom 48%.
- Only these two people. No dog, no buildings, no fences, no signs, no poppies.
- The grass a rich dry gold, streaked by the wind; the child's shirt the one note of soft blue.

Paint a NEW picture. Loose, quick ink brush strokes, light washes, lots of bare paper: figures, not portraits, no faces drawn.
