TWO PAINTINGS ARE ATTACHED. The first (a grandfather swinging a small child round by both hands on a golden California hillside, both laughing) is the picture to paint again: the same two people, the same clothes and colours, the same moment, place and light, the same loose, watery hand. The second (the Big Sur coast) gives only the way of painting.

THE PICTURE: the first painting again as a TALL picture for a phone's screen, where the words "Why start with the cell? Everything you do runs on energy your cells make." stand in its sky. Portrait, 1024 x 1536.

THE SCENE: as in the first painting. A bright afternoon on rolling golden California hills, a pale blue-grey coast range on the horizon. The grandfather (pale straw sun hat, pale shirt with the sleeves rolled, khaki trousers) stands with his feet planted wide, leaning back, laughing, and swings the child (soft blue t-shirt, tan shorts, no hat) round by both hands; the child flies out level beside him, legs streaming out behind, laughing. The child's little sun hat tumbles in the air above. Their joined hands are plain to see between them. Faces in a few loose strokes only, as in the first painting.

SIZE AND PLACE (important):
- The top 46% of the picture is open sky: bare paper with only the palest warm wash, nothing in it at all (no clouds, no sun, no birds), so that large dark words can sit there. Only the tumbling hat may be just below it, at about 50% down.
- The far range lies along the horizon at about 60% down.
- The grandfather is about 30% of the picture's height: the top of his hat at about 58% down, his feet at about 88% down. He stands at about 38% across. The child flies out to his right, the child's body between about 50% and 78% across, at about 64% to 72% down: the child is small, a five-year-old, and swung round a little toward us, so the legs do not reach far. The phone shows only the middle of this picture: EVERYTHING of the two people (his hat brim, his back, his shoes, the child's shoes, the tumbling hat) stays between 24% and 78% across. The outer 22% at the left and the right is only grass and sky.

COMPOSITION:
- A dirt trail and dry grass under them; a few California poppies near the bottom left.
- The land runs off the left, right and bottom edges with no fade and no vignette.
- Only these two people. No dog, no buildings, no fences, no signs.
