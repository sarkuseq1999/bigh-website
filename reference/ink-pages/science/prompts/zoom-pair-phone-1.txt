TWO PAINTINGS ARE ATTACHED. The first (a grandfather and a small child walking hand in hand on a trail through golden California hills) is the picture to paint again: the same two people, the same clothes and colours, the same place and light, the same loose hand. The second (the Big Sur coast) gives only the way of painting.

THE PICTURE: the first painting again as a TALL picture for a phone's screen, where the words "Why start with the cell? Everything you do runs on energy your cells make." stand in its sky. Portrait, 1024 x 1536.

THE SCENE: as in the first painting. Late morning on rolling golden California hills: dry grass, a valley oak on the ridge, a pale blue-grey coast range on the horizon. On a narrow dirt trail, seen from behind and a little to one side, the grandfather (sun hat, pale shirt with the sleeves rolled, khaki trousers) and the small child (little sun hat, soft blue t-shirt, tan shorts) walk away from us hand in hand, in mid-stride; the child's free arm is lifted, pointing ahead up the trail. Their joined hands are plain to see between them. Figures, not portraits: no faces drawn. Painted in sure ink strokes and a few light washes.

SIZE AND PLACE (important):
- The top 42% of the picture is open sky: bare paper with only the palest warm wash, nothing in it at all (no clouds, no sun, no birds, no branches), so that large dark words can sit there.
- The far range lies along the horizon at about 46% down.
- The grandfather is about 36% of the picture's height: the top of his hat at about 50% down, his feet at about 86% down. The child stands at his right, as in the first painting.
- The pair stand in the middle of the picture: together they fill the width from about 30% to 72% across. Nothing that matters is in the outer 15% at the left or the right.
- The oak is small, on the ridge at the right, its crown below 44% down.

COMPOSITION:
- The trail comes up from the bottom edge to the pair's feet and runs on up the slope to the right beyond them.
- Dry grass and two or three California poppies near the bottom left.
- The land runs off the left, right and bottom edges with no fade and no vignette.
- Only these two people. No dog, no buildings, no fences, no signs.

Paint a NEW picture. Loose, quick ink brush strokes, light washes, lots of bare paper.
