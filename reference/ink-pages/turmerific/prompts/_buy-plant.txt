A turmeric plant in flower, painted large. From the lower left and lower right, its long, broad, lance-shaped leaves rise and arch outward in a loose fan, in fresh sage-green and soft grey-green washes with darker ink strokes for the midribs, some leaves overlapping, their tips curving gently. Rising from the back on the right, above the leaves, one turmeric flower spike: a soft cone of overlapping pale green bracts that turn a delicate rose pink toward the top, with a few small pale yellow flowers peeking out between the lower bracts. At the foot of the plant, a soft, pale grey wash shadow on the ground across the lower part of the picture. In the middle of the lower half, between the leaves, an EMPTY space of plain paper, about a third of the picture wide and half of its height tall, where an object will stand later: nothing is painted there except the soft ground wash at its bottom; no pot, no vase, no bottle, no jar. A few fallen round slices of turmeric root, their cut faces real gold leaf, lie on the ground wash at the front, either side of that empty space. Fresh, calm, generous.
