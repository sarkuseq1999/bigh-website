STYLE: An ink-and-wash painting on plain white paper, in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters. The land is first drawn and shaded with a brush in grey and black ink, quickly and loosely, with dry-brush strokes; then a few light, transparent watercolour washes are laid over it. The sky is one big wet wash with no ink in it. The ink does the work on the land; the colour is light and calm. Lots of bare paper. You can see every brush stroke; soft bleeding edges where the paper was wet, crisp ones where it was dry. Richness comes from brushwork and ink, never from fine or photographic detail. The more it looks like a painting by a grown-up painter, the better. Not photo-real, no smooth digital gradients, no pen outlines, not a storybook or greeting-card illustration, not a cartoon. An American, California scene, NOT an Asian ink painting: no misty peaks, no bands of fog, no pine tree clinging to a rock, no cranes, no boats.

THE PICTURE: the opening painting of a product page: a turmeric field on a small Southern California farm (Ventura County) in the warm, clear light of late afternoon, and almost all of it is SKY. Large dark lettering and a product will be laid over it later, so the painting must stay simple, calm and light. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

COMPOSITION, top to bottom (keep these heights):
- From the top edge down to 60% of the height: open sky. One big wet wash of clear cerulean blue: mid-pale at the top, much paler toward the hills and touched there with a thin warm golden haze, uneven as a real wash is, with granulation, brush direction showing, and one or two soft backruns. Two small soft clouds left as bare white paper, high up near the top corners only. No sun, no birds.
- From 60% to 68%: far, low chaparral hills across the whole width in light washes of sage and warm ochre, their top a gentle low line that never rises above 60% of the height; along the farm's far edge a long row of tall eucalyptus trees as a windbreak, drawn small and loose in grey ink with blue-green washes. Calm and pale.
- From 68% to 75%: the nearest rows of the turmeric field in the foreground, from the left edge to 40% across and from 60% across to the right edge, drawn quickly in grey ink with green washes. Turmeric plants: clumps of tall, broad, lance-shaped leaves on short stems, fresh bright green with darker veins, about knee high, a few clumps with a pale pink-and-white flower spike low among their leaves. The warm brown earth between them in light washes. Near the inner ends of the rows, a few freshly dug turmeric roots lie on the earth, their cut ends bright orange-gold. In the very middle of the picture (from 42% to 58% across) the ground is flat, low and bare, only warm earth and a few dry-brush specks: an EMPTY spot where an object will be placed later. Nothing stands there.
- From 75% to 80%: the earth thins out downward into a few loose dry-brush strokes and specks.
- The bottom 20% of the picture: completely bare white paper. Nothing there.

EDGES: the sky, the hills and the foreground run off the left and right edges, and the sky off the top edge, with no fade, no vignette and no border. The bottom ends in bare white paper, never in a straight painted line.

NEVER: no text, letters or numbers anywhere, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold paint or gold leaf, no bottles, jars, glasses or products, no pills, capsules or powder, no people, no animals, no buildings except where asked, no road, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo, no sun disc.
