A short, knobbly piece of fresh turmeric root lying diagonally in the lower left, its thin skin a few dry-brush strokes of warm brown-grey ink with the faint rings of the root, its freshly cut end pointing up and to the right, the cut face bright, real gold leaf. From that cut end, a long, gentle, curving trail of many tiny round droplets of real gold leaf drifts upward and to the right across the paper, like a slow stream of tiny golden beads: close together and a little larger near the root, then smaller, sparser and fainter as they travel toward the upper right corner. Each droplet a single small round dab of gold, some with a faint golden halo where it touched the wet paper. Nothing else. Calm, light, luminous.
