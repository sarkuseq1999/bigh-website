Brush calligraphy of numbers only, written in one row across the middle of the paper in sumi ink by a master's steady, controlled hand: the number 2, then a small multiplication sign (×), then the number 30, then a small equals sign (=), then the number 60. It reads exactly: 2 × 30 = 60. Leave clear space between every numeral and sign; nothing touches. Each numeral bold, refined and perfectly legible (clear Western numerals, never stylised into other shapes), with wet strokes and a little dry-brush at the ends; the two signs smaller and lighter. Nothing else on the paper: no other characters, no words, no gold.
