California golden hills in the late afternoon. Soft, rolling hills of dry golden grass fill the lower half, painted in warm ochre and pale gold washes with darker ink strokes along their curves, a little real gold leaf where the low sun catches the grass on the ridges. On a far ridge on the left, one lone valley oak with a broad, rounded crown in loose dark ink strokes, small in the distance. Above, a pale, warm sky of bare paper with the faintest wash. In the foreground across the bottom, a few tall blades of golden grass in quick strokes, and a soft pale ground wash. In the middle of the lower half, an EMPTY space of plain paper and soft ground wash, about a third of the picture wide and half of its height tall, where an object will stand later: nothing is painted there; no tree, no rock, no bottle, no jar, no post. Wide, warm, quiet.
