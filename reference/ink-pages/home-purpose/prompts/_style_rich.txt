STYLE: A finished, gallery-quality California plein-air watercolour with touches of ink, on warm white paper. Mo, who owns this website, found the last version of this painting "a little simple" next to the old closing painting it replaced (the first attached picture). Match that old painting's level of finish, depth and light, NOT its subject: none of its pine tree, its rocks, its sea of mist or its pointed mountains. What makes it look like a real picture, and what this one needs:
- Deep space: many layers receding into the evening haze, with atmospheric perspective. The nearest things are darker, warmer and more detailed; each layer behind is paler, cooler and softer, the farthest a pale blue-grey wash.
- A full range of tones, from a few deep ink accents in the foreground to bare paper in the sky.
- Fine, confident brushwork up close (grasses, poppies, rocks, the trail); soft wet washes in the distance.
- Warm, low evening light: it rakes across the hills, edges the cliffs in gold, and glitters on the sea under the sun.
It must still look painted by hand: soft bleeding edges, paper showing through, no photo-realism, no digital look, no pen outlines.

It is an American, California scene, NOT an Asian ink painting: no pine trees, no cypress trees, no pointed misty peaks, no sea of mist or bands of fog, no pagodas, no cranes, no characters, no seals or stamps.

THE PEOPLE (from the second attached picture): an older couple and their dog walking away from us along a cliff-top trail, small and far (the people about 5% of the picture's height), each painted with a few touches, no faces, no details.

COMPOSITION (it is the wide painting that closes a website's homepage; only a wide band of it will be shown): Landscape, 1536 x 1024.
- The top 50% of the picture is open evening sky: bare paper with at most a very pale warm wash near the horizon. No clouds, no birds, no sun disc. Big words will be set there.
- The sea's horizon runs level at 66% down.
- A gold-leaf sun will be added later, sitting on the horizon at 81% across. Keep that spot clear, with only a pale warm glow around it.
- Land and sea run off both the left and the right edge, with no fade at the sides; the land may rise to 48% down at the far left, never higher.
- Below 92% down everything fades softly into bare paper; no hard edge, no frame.

NEVER: no sun disc, no text, letters or numbers, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold, no birds, no cars, no buildings unless named below.
