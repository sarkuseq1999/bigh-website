STYLE: A loose ink-and-watercolour landscape on plain warm white paper, by the same painter as the attached giant sequoia painting: the same wet washes with soft bleeding edges, a few dry-brush streaks, soft calm colours, lots of bare paper. The more it looks like a painting, the better. Not photo-real, not a detailed drawing, not a storybook illustration, no pen outlines.

It is an American, California scene in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters, NOT an Asian ink painting: no pointed misty peaks, no sea of mist or layered fog, no twisted pine or lone tree clinging to a rock, no cranes, no pagodas, no characters, no seals or stamps.

COMPOSITION (it is the wide painting that closes a website's homepage; only a wide band of it will be shown): Landscape, 1536 x 1024.
- The top 55% of the picture is empty bare paper sky: no clouds, no birds, no sun disc. Big words will be set there, and a gold-leaf sun will be added later.
- The horizon runs across the full width at 67% down, level.
- A gold-leaf sun will be added later, sitting on the horizon at 81% across. Keep that spot clear, with only a very pale warm peach wash around it fading into the paper, like evening light.
- The land and sea run off both the left and the right edge of the picture, with no fade at the sides.
- Everything nearer than the horizon fits between 67% and 92% down, except where the subject below says otherwise. Below that it fades softly into bare paper; no hard edge, no frame.

NEVER: no sun disc, no text, letters or numbers, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold, no lab glassware, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo.

THE SCENE: A long golden California ridge on a clear evening, with no trees on it. Rounded golden-grass hills in broad wet washes of straw ochre, rolling across the whole width, nearer hills a little darker with sage in the folds, far hills pale blue-grey at the horizon. A thin pale trail runs along the near ridge. On that trail, at about 58% across and 76% down, two people walking side by side with a dog beside them, seen from behind and far away, very small (the people about 4% of the picture's height), each painted with just two or three touches of dark ink, no faces, no details. They look like an older couple out for an evening walk. No birds, no buildings, no fences, no poppies. Paint it even looser: fewer strokes, the hills almost one wash each.
