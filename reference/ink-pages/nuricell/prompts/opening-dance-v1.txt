An older couple, both with grey hair, dancing a joyful swing dance, painted in a few loose, fast, expressive strokes, full of movement. He leads in shirt sleeves and slacks, one arm raised; she spins out from his hand, her full skirt flaring in a wide swirl of soft grey wash; both are light on their feet, mid-step. Faces are only a soft dab of pale wash, no features. One ribbon of real gold leaf runs along the hem of her flaring skirt, following the spin: the only gold in the picture. A short, soft grey wash under their feet for the floor. The two figures fill most of the picture's height. Nothing else: no room, no band, no other people, no lamps, no text.
