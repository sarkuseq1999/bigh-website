THE PICTURE: only a SHADOW. The soft morning shadow of two California fan-palm and feather-palm fronds falling across a sheet of plain white paper. The palms themselves are not in the picture, only their shadow. It will be laid over a web page as light, so it must be soft, pale and calm. Landscape, 1536 x 1024, greys only.

THE SHADOW: painted like a pale grey ink wash on wet paper, or seen like a real out-of-focus shadow: soft edges everywhere, no outlines, no detail. Low morning sun comes from the upper right, so the fronds reach in from the top right corner and their shadow spreads down and to the left.
- It is densest in the upper right third of the picture (the long thin leaflets of the fronds, a mid warm grey).
- Toward the middle the leaflets are fewer and blurrier, with wide gaps of white sunlight between them.
- The lower left third of the picture is almost clear: only one or two very faint, very blurred leaflet tips, then pure white.
- Some leaflets are a little sharper and some very soft, as leaves at different distances are.

THE PAPER: pure plain white everywhere the shadow does not fall. No paper texture, no wall, no window frame, no bars, no objects.

NEVER: no tree trunk, no real leaves in colour, no green, no bamboo, no text, letters or numbers, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold, no hard black shapes, no glow.
