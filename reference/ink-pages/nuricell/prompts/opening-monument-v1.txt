A traditional ink-wash painting (sumi-e) on one plain sheet of warm, light rice paper with fine fibres, evenly lit, no frame, no text, no signature, no red seal, no stamp. Paint it the way a master ink painter works: a few confident, loose brush strokes, wet ink bleeding softly into the paper, dry-brush texture where the brush runs out, simplified forms that suggest rather than describe, lots of white paper. It must look like a painting, expressive and artistic, with the brushwork of the reference picture: never a realistic drawing, never a detailed illustration, never a pencil sketch, never a 3D render. Sumi ink only, from deep black to soft grey washes, plus real gold leaf only where stated. Copy only the reference's brushwork, never its subject: no bird, no crane, no reeds, no water, no mountains, no sun. A wide picture, about one and a half times as wide as it is tall.

A playful scene with a surprising sense of scale, like a monument in a park. In the right half of the picture stands a plain, low block of pale grey granite with two shallow steps, its flat top empty; its top edge is about two thirds of the way down the picture, and its width is about a quarter of the picture's width. Above its top there is a tall empty space reaching almost to the top edge of the picture: keep that space completely empty, nothing painted there (a tall object about three times the block's height will stand on it later). Around the block, three small older people, each about as tall as the block, painted in a few loose, lively strokes, faces only a soft dab of pale wash: at the left foot of the block, a woman in a wide sun hat waters a clump of California poppies with a watering can; on the lower step, a grey-haired man sits reading a book; at the right side, a woman stands on a short wooden stepladder leaning against the block, reaching up with a soft cloth toward the empty space as if polishing something tall that will stand there, her hand ending at the edge of the empty space. The poppies are real gold leaf with softly torn leaf edges: the only gold in the picture. A short soft grey wash for the ground. Nothing else: no trees, no buildings, no sky, no landscape, no other objects.
