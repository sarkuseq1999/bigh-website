STYLE: A loose watercolour painting on plain white paper, in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters. Wet washes laid quickly with a big brush, soft bleeding edges where the paper was wet, crisp ones where it was dry, a little grey ink brush drawing only in the grass and rocks. Lots of bare paper. You can see every brush stroke. The more it looks like a painting, the better. Not photo-real, no fine detail, no smooth digital gradients, no pen outlines, not a storybook illustration, not a cartoon. An American, California scene, NOT an Asian ink painting: no misty peaks, no bands of fog, no pine tree clinging to a rock, no cranes, no boats.

THE PICTURE: the opening painting of a product page: a clear morning on the California coast, and almost all of it is SKY. Large dark lettering and a product will be laid over it later, so the painting must stay simple, calm and light. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

COMPOSITION, top to bottom (keep these heights):
- From the top edge down to 60% of the height: open sky. One big wet wash of clear cerulean blue, the blue of a cloudless California morning leaning a little toward cyan: mid-pale at the top, paler toward the horizon, uneven as a real wash is, with granulation and one or two soft backruns. Two or three small soft clouds left as bare white paper, high up near the top corners only. No sun, no birds, no hard-edged clouds.
- From 60% to 68%: the Pacific, one flat band of deeper blue (a little ultramarine and teal), its horizon a clean, level, slightly soft line across the whole width. A few bare-paper flecks of light on the water. No boats, no rocks in the sea, no islands.
- From 68% to 75%: the top of a low grassy coastal bluff in the foreground, running across the whole width like a low rolling line, a little higher toward the left and right edges. Dry golden grass in light gold-ochre washes with a few quick grey ink strokes, three or four small orange California poppies near the left third and the right third, one or two small grey stones. In the very middle of the picture (from 42% to 58% across) the bluff's top is flat, low and bare, only short grass: an EMPTY spot where an object will be placed later. Nothing stands there.
- From 75% to 80%: the bluff's grass thins out downward into a few loose dry-brush strokes and specks.
- The bottom 20% of the picture: completely bare white paper. Nothing there.

EDGES: the sky, the sea and the bluff run off the left and right edges, and the sky off the top edge, with no fade, no vignette and no border. The bottom ends in bare white paper, never in a straight painted line.

NEVER: no text, letters or numbers anywhere, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold paint, no bottles, jars or products, no pills or capsules, no people, no animals, no buildings, no bridge, no lighthouse, no trees, no fence, no path, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo.
