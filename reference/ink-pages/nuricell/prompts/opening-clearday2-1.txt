STYLE: An ink-and-wash painting on plain white paper, in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters. The land is first drawn and shaded with a brush in grey and black ink, quickly and loosely, with dry-brush strokes; then a few light, transparent watercolour washes are laid over it. The sky is one big wet wash with no ink in it. The ink does the work on the land; the colour is light and calm. Lots of bare paper. You can see every brush stroke; soft bleeding edges where the paper was wet, crisp ones where it was dry. Richness comes from brushwork and ink, never from fine or photographic detail. The more it looks like a painting by a grown-up painter, the better. Not photo-real, no smooth digital gradients, no pen outlines, not a storybook or greeting-card illustration, not a cartoon. An American, California scene, NOT an Asian ink painting: no misty peaks, no bands of fog, no pine tree clinging to a rock, no cranes, no boats.

THE PICTURE: the opening painting of a product page: a clear morning on the California coast, and almost all of it is SKY. Large dark lettering and a product will be laid over it later, so the painting must stay simple, calm and light. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

COMPOSITION, top to bottom (keep these heights):
- From the top edge down to 60% of the height: open sky. One big wet wash of clear cerulean blue, the blue of a cloudless California morning leaning a little toward cyan: mid-pale at the top, much paler toward the horizon, uneven as a real wash is, with granulation, brush direction showing, and one or two soft backruns. Two small soft clouds left as bare white paper, high up near the top corners only. No sun, no birds, no hard-edged clouds.
- From 60% to 68%: the Pacific, one band of deeper blue (ultramarine with a little teal), darker at the horizon, its horizon a clean, level line across the whole width. A few dry-brush flecks of bare paper for light on the water and two or three thin ink lines for swell. No boats, no rocks in the sea, no islands.
- From 68% to 75%: the top of a low grassy coastal bluff in the foreground, running across the whole width as a low rolling line, a little higher toward the left and right edges. Dry golden grass: quick upward ink brush strokes of different greys, bent by the wind, with light gold-ochre and a little sage washed over them. Four or five SMALL orange California poppies as single dabs, low in the grass near the left quarter and the right quarter, none bigger than a grass tuft. One weathered grey stone at each side. In the very middle of the picture (from 42% to 58% across) the bluff's top is flat, low and bare, only short grass: an EMPTY spot where an object will be placed later. Nothing stands there.
- From 75% to 80%: the grass thins out downward into a few loose dry-brush strokes and specks.
- The bottom 20% of the picture: completely bare white paper. Nothing there.

EDGES: the sky, the sea and the bluff run off the left and right edges, and the sky off the top edge, with no fade, no vignette and no border. The bottom ends in bare white paper, never in a straight painted line.

NEVER: no text, letters or numbers anywhere, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold paint, no bottles, jars or products, no pills or capsules, no people, no animals, no buildings, no bridge, no lighthouse, no trees, no fence, no path, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo.
