Paint a single finished editorial watercolor illustration for the ingredient research chapter of a California natural science website. A real, sunlit academic reading-room sill in the Berkeley hills, as an American West Coast watercolorist might paint it from life. Seven beautiful broad clothbound field journals stand side by side on a honey-redwood sill, looking out into a California coast live oak, tawny summer grass and two pale overlapping East Bay hills. A fragment of a simple timber sash at the extreme left, open air beyond; no full architectural frame. Low oblique morning sun, cool lavender-blue shadows, open paper highlights. This is a QUIET illustrated spot vignette, intimate and tactile; avoid poster graphics, hard enclosing borders or decorative framing.
Composition: horizontal 5:4 illustration, subjects comfortably inside. Upper half: one beautifully gestural, asymmetrical live oak to the left, hills sweeping into mostly empty sky on the upper right. Lower half: the books are the heroes, large enough to tap. EXACTLY SEVEN separate hefty closed journals, all seven in a single horizontal row, the broad spine of each clearly facing the viewer, resting on the SAME ledge. As seen almost straight on with a little top face visible. Together the books span almost the whole painted width (from 10% to 90% of canvas), and have slightly varied heights and character. First book a deep indigo blue, then faded sage, redwood rust, weathered teal, California buckskin, slate blue, and olive. Mostly upright with the last one leaning a little to the right. Their spines have NO lettering, titles, labels, numbers or horizontal ornamental rules. No extra books or open book; no objects crowding the journals. Pigment catches on linen; small accents of warm sepia pencil, with loose wet washes, lost-and-found edges and luminous white paper. Sunlit land softly subordinate to the books. Around ALL outer edges let paint break and dissolve irregularly all the way into pure white unpainted paper, generous white corners; the paper will be removed when placed on the website. A beautiful restrained hand painting, not a flat icon, not a sepia antique or glossy digital image. No gold, no lettering or signatures, no logos, no flags, no human figures, no UI.
