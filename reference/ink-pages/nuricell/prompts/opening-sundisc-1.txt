STYLE: A watercolour painting on plain white paper, in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters: one big wet wash for the sky, painted quickly and loosely; the sea a band of deeper blue with dry-brush flecks of bare paper. You can see every brush stroke; soft bleeding edges where the paper was wet, crisp ones where it was dry. Not photo-real, no smooth digital gradients, no airbrush, no lens flare, not a greeting-card illustration, not a cartoon sun.

THE ATTACHED PAINTING is the picture this one will be laid over: take its sky's clear cerulean blue, its sea's deeper blue, its granulation and its way of painting.

THE PICTURE: the right-hand part of that same clear California morning over the Pacific, with the morning sun in the sky. Landscape, 1536 x 1024.

- From the top down to 78% of the height: sky, one big wet wash of the attached painting's clear cerulean blue, uneven, granulating.
- The sun: high in the top right, its middle about 12% in from the right edge and 14% down from the top. It is simply a round of bare white paper about 8% of the picture's width across, left unpainted; its rim is soft and a little broken where the wet wash crept in, never a drawn line or a perfect circle. Around it the blue was lifted while wet, and a warm wash of very pale Naples yellow, with a breath of cadmium yellow light, bleeds out wet-in-wet into the blue, reaching about a quarter of the picture's width from the sun and fading out unevenly with soft blooms. No rays, no beams.
- From 78% to 90% of the height: the Pacific, a band of deeper blue (ultramarine with a little teal) like the attached painting's sea, its horizon a clean level line at 78%. Under the sun, the sun's reflection on the water: a path of short horizontal flecks of bare white paper and a few touches of the same pale yellow, left by a dry brush dragged sideways, many and close together just under the horizon below the sun, spreading a little wider and sparser toward the bottom of the band. The rest of the sea has only a few flecks.
- The bottom 10%: bare white paper.

NEVER: no clouds, no birds, no boats, no land, no rocks, no grass, no text, letters or numbers, no signature, no seal, no frame or border, no gold paint, no orange or red, no face on the sun, no sparkle stars, no lens flare.
