STYLE: A painting for a California brand's website, in loose ink and thin watercolour on plain warm white paper. Use the attached paintings only as the reference for the brush hand and the palette (do not paint what they show): the same painter, the same few wet washes with soft bleeding edges, a few dry-brush streaks, very few strokes, lots of bare paper. The more it looks like a painting, the better. Not photo-real, not a detailed drawing, not a cartoon or children's-book illustration, no pen outlines.

LIGHT AND WARM: this picture replaces one that looked dark and a little scary, so keep it light, sunny and friendly. Thin, pale colour: sage and olive greens, warm straw and ochre, a little pale sky blue, and soft warm grey ink only for a few lines and shadows. No black, no heavy dark masses, no bare or twisted branches, nothing gloomy, lonely or empty.

PEOPLE (only where the order asks for them): small, loose figures in a few brush strokes each, like the walkers in the attached coast painting. No drawn faces, no detail, relaxed and happy poses, everyday clothes in pale washes.

GOLD: where the order says gold leaf, lay it as flat, matte gold leaf, like a gilded page: an even warm gold with a slightly broken edge, no shine, no glow, no sparkle, no halo, no rays. The gold appears ONLY where the order says. Nothing else in the picture is gold or bright yellow.

PAPER AND EDGES: plain warm white paper all round. The painting floats in the middle of the sheet as a vignette whose soft, irregular edges fade into bare paper on all four sides. Nothing touches the edge of the picture. No frame, no border, no hard rectangle of colour.

It is an American, California picture, in the spirit of the California plein-air watercolour painters, NOT an Asian ink painting: no scroll, no pagoda, no bamboo, no twisted pine on a rock, no characters, no seal or stamp.

NEVER: no text, letters, numbers or writing anywhere, no signature, no lab glassware, no pills or capsules, no powder, no jars or bottles, no red or rust clustered dots, no glow, no halo, no sparkle.
