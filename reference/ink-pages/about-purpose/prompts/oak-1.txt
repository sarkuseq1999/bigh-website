COMPOSITION: Square, 1024 x 1024. A sunny afternoon under one big California valley oak, seen from the side, with the ground cut away beneath it like a page from a nature book.
- The upper two thirds: the oak. A broad, round, leafy crown in soft sage and olive green washes, with sunlight (bare paper) breaking through it. A sturdy, gently leaning trunk in warm grey-brown. Full and healthy: NOT gnarled, NOT twisted, no bare branches.
- From a strong low branch hangs a rope swing with a child on it in mid-swing, legs out. A grandparent stands just behind, arms out, having just pushed. A small dog sits in the grass watching. On the other side of the trunk, a picnic blanket with a basket and a straw hat. All of it small and loose, a few strokes each.
- The ground: a gentle rise of dry golden summer grass in warm straw washes, with a few dry-brush grass strokes.
- The lower third, below the grass line: the earth cut away as a soft, warm, pale brown-ochre wash, like a slice of soil, widest under the trunk and fading out into bare paper at the sides and at the bottom. Inside this warm earth the oak's roots spread as a few soft, rounded, pale grey-brown strokes, gentle, like the branches of a river. The roots stay INSIDE the earth wash. They never hang in empty paper and are never thin, spiky or claw-like.
- GOLD LEAF: right under the trunk, among the roots, one irregular patch of flat matte gold leaf with a slightly broken edge, about a tenth of the picture's width, the roots passing round it. This is the only gold in the picture.

Mood: a warm, happy family afternoon. The swing is in use. Nothing empty, dark or lonely.

No buildings, no fences, no cars, no birds.
