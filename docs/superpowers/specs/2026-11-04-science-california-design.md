## The story (one idea per screen)

The live page (October 26) opened on "Meet the scientists behind BiGH's key formulas." and Dr.
Liu, then his path (Okayama, Berkeley, Salzburg, Today), research, health explained, formulas,
Ask. A visitor met his record before knowing what he works on. New order:

1. **What we study.** "Our science starts in the cell." The two questions (how cells make energy;
   what changes as we age) and the scientist who has spent his career on them, by name, on the
   first screen (Mo's September rule: real scientists in the first impression).
2. **Why the cell.** Everything you do runs on energy your cells make; about 37 trillion cells,
   each making its own. Three numbered lines (mitochondria; free radicals; aging), each opening
   the kit's existing explainer sheet. This replaces "Make sense of the science." (the same
   heading as the homepage's science section; a repeat on the site).
3. **Dr. Liu.** His real photograph on its mat, name, role, the approved intro, his story sheet.
4. **His path.** Berkeley 1994 (the Okayama doctorate told in its words), Salzburg 2025, Today.
5. **The formulas.** "Every formula has a scientist behind it." The five bottles and credits.
6. **The research.** Three studies to start with, all 36 sources on request, the limits note.
   Its heading is new: "Curiosity, with references." is the homepage's research heading.
7. **Ask BiGH Science.** Unchanged words; still in development.

Formulas move before the research: "Today: his research now guides the science behind our
formulas." leads straight into the bottles, and reading the research leads into asking.
