## 3. The Science Story (this is the heart of the page)

NuriCell is built on a formula known in the research literature as **CellGen**,
developed by **Dr. Jian Kang Liu**, a biochemist who did his landmark work in
the **Division of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology at the University of
California, Berkeley**, alongside the renowned scientist **Dr. Bruce Ames**.

The core idea — the **mitochondrial theory of brain aging**:
- Mitochondria are the power plants inside every cell. They make the energy
  (ATP) that thinking, remembering, and focusing all depend on.
- With age, mitochondria **decay** — oxidative damage builds up in their lipids,
  proteins, RNA and DNA. They make less energy and leak more damage.
- This decline tracks with the memory loss and mental fatigue of aging.

**Dr. Liu's landmark finding (published in PNAS, 2002):** feeding two
mitochondrial nutrients — **acetyl-L-carnitine and R-alpha-lipoic acid** — to
old rats **partially reversed** age-associated brain mitochondrial decay and
**improved their performance on memory tasks**. The combination worked better
than either nutrient alone. Electron-microscope imaging showed aged mitochondria
structurally restored toward a younger state.

This research was published across the most respected journals in the field —
**Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS)**, the **FASEB
Journal**, and the **Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences** — and the
broader work on mitochondrial aging drew coverage from **the BBC** and
**Discover** magazine.

NuriCell takes that research and builds it into a daily capsule: the two studied
mitochondrial nutrients, plus two more that support the same energy-and-memory
system.

## 4. The Formula — 4 Active Ingredients

Serving size = 2 capsules. Amounts below are **per daily serving**.

| Ingredient | Amount | Short name | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| N-Acetyl L-Carnitine HCl | 267 mg | **The Carrier** | A mitochondrial nutrient that helps shuttle fuel into the cell's power plants and crosses the blood–brain barrier. Studied for memory and mental energy. |
| Creatine Monohydrate | 267 mg | **The Reservoir** | A rechargeable energy buffer (phosphocreatine) that keeps ATP available for fast thinking. Human trials link it to better working memory and processing speed. |
| Alpha-Lipoic Acid | 133 mg | **The Protector** | A mitochondrial antioxidant that neutralizes oxidative damage and regenerates other antioxidants (vitamins C and E, glutathione). The partner that makes the Carrier work better. |
| Choline Bitartrate | 67 mg | **The Messenger** | A building block of acetylcholine — the neurotransmitter the brain uses for memory and learning — and of healthy brain-cell membranes. |

**The synergy story:** acetyl-L-carnitine and alpha-lipoic acid are the two
nutrients from Dr. Liu's research, and they were shown to work *better together
than alone*. Creatine and choline extend the same energy-and-memory system.
The formula is a system, not a pile of ingredients.
