# Homepage, look "Ink & Gold"

Scope: the BiGH homepage (`/?look=ink` crane opening, `/?look=ink-cell` cell opening), desktop and
phone, five languages. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: midlife buyers (40–65) deciding whether to trust BiGH; within ten seconds they
must believe real cell scientists stand behind these formulas (PRODUCT.md), then go to NuriCell.
Proof on hand: Dr. Jiankang Liu's real photo and record (280+ papers, 2016, 20+ years), the 36
checked research sources, the approved bottle photos. Constraints: today's words verbatim
(content.ts), real assets only, honesty labels kept, large navigation.

Chosen direction: Ink & Gold (concept A), approved by Mo on October 2, 2026 as two openings to
compare: 2 "the crane" (my pick) and 1 "the cell". Approved comps:
`.impeccable/mocks/approved/ink-crane-hero.png` + `ink-crane-page.png` and
`.impeccable/mocks/approved/ink-cell-hero.png` + `ink-cell-page.png`. Picture model for plates:
GPT Image 2.5 (Mo's head-to-head, October 2).

Memorable moment: one ink brush line that draws itself down the whole page as you scroll, from the
crane's flight path (or the cell) through every section.

Unresolved: which opening Mo keeps; translations of any new labels.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The long life you want and the cell that powers it, painted in one breath of ink with a
single gold stroke for energy. It refuses the category default: a lifestyle photo over a product
shelf on cream, with cards and gradients.

OWN-WORLD: Warm rice paper (#F6F3EC) with real fibre; sumi ink (#1C1B19) and ink-wash greys; one
gold leaf (#C49A3A) only for energy (the sun, the glow inside the cell). Switzer headlines,
large and calm; hairline labels pinned to the painting with fine leader lines. No cards, no
gradients, no red, no seals: paintings, paper, type and one continuous brush line.

STORY: The visitor sees the crane of long life (or their own cell), learns mitochondria are tiny
power plants, meets Dr. Liu and his record, finds the five products standing on ink-wash shadows,
then the stories, the science, the research and the purpose. They believe real scientists stand
behind BiGH and press Discover NuriCell.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Crane: a full-width ink landscape (misty mountains, crane in flight upper left of
centre, gold sun upper right); the three-line headline at left in the lower half, intro under it,
two pill buttons; the brush line leaves the crane and falls off the right edge. Cell: headline
upper left, the ink mitochondrion filling the right half with three hairline labels, buttons
under the intro, the brush line leaving the cell's foot.

FORM: East Asian ink-wash painting on rice paper, candidate 6 of my ordered list (assigned by the
roll), with the crane from the competitive challenger (origami crane, long life) translated into
ink; seed key bc6a2814.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
