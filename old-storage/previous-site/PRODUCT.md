# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Korean / Asian families (direct-sales, Korea-led) buying premium health as a **gift for the people they love** (효도 / filial care). Their goal is **well-aging (슬로우 에이징)** — not anti-aging, not biohacking. Secondary: US visitors ("sharp mind, steady energy, honestly") and BiGH's own direct-sales sellers, who need a site they can trust and share. Six locales (EN/CN/KR/JP/HK/VN).

## Product Purpose

The public home of BiGH ("Be in Good Health") supplements — a **trust engine**: one real scientist (Dr. Jiankang Liu, mitochondrial-nutrient researcher, EASA member), honest formulas, gentle daily doses designed for a lifetime. Hero product = NuriCell (cellular/brain energy; same formula trusted for ~20 years as CellGen). Success = a visitor feels "these people are honest" and starts the ritual — or gifts it.

## Brand Personality

Warm, honest, premium-calm. **"The science of aging well, made with heart."** The feeling: *"Age well, together."* We sell the RITUAL (one gentle capsule a day), the PERSON (Dr. Liu), and the LOVE (for the people you love) — never claims.

## Anti-references

- Biohacker / longevity-startup aesthetics (Blueprint, clinical dashboards, cold labs).
- Prevagen-style brain-boost promises; any "improves memory / anti-aging / reverses aging" language (see guardrails below).
- Supplement-hype pages: 10× claims, megadose bravado, urgency timers, MLM opportunity-first framing.
- Generic AI landing-page grammar: cream+terracotta template, eyebrow-kicker on every section, identical card grids, hero-metric blocks.

## Design Principles

1. **Never outrun the evidence.** Every visual and line stays inside structure/function language; honesty IS the aesthetic.
2. **The reveal is the Dr. Liu moment.** Brain = emotional hook, cellular energy = the reveal; design choreography should stage hook → reveal as one message.
3. **Gentle dose is a virtue.** Calm, unhurried pacing; nothing shouts. "Not the biggest dose — the right one."
4. **Warmth over hype.** Golden-hour warmth (ember accents, serif elegance) carries "made with heart"; data appears as poetry, not dashboard.
5. **Show the mechanism honestly.** Cellular energy visualized truthfully (e.g. real proportions like 2% body / 20% energy) beats decorative science imagery.

## Compliance guardrails (hard)

No disease words (Alzheimer's, dementia, …); no "boosts/sharpens memory" promises; no anti-aging/lifespan claims; no free-radical cure narrative; Berkeley/Ames = factual backdrop with citations, never endorsement; DSHEA disclaimer stays. Full rules: Dropbox `bigh-website/materials/Brand-and-Strategy/Positioning-Statement.md` (the north star).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduced motion honored on every animation (crossfade/instant fallback — pattern already in the codebase). Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast on both the dark (`.gh`) and any light scopes. CJK locales use Noto companion faces; layouts must survive longer CJK strings. Keyboard focus visible on all interactive chrome.
