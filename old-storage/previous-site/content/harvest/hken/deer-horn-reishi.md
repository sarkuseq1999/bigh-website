## Deer Horn Reishi

Once reserved only for the wealthy and royals dated back almost 3,000 years ago in Asia, Red Reishi mushrooms (Ganoderma Lucidum) was known as “superior herb” and “mushroom of longevity” by locals. BiGH’s Deer Horn Reishi is a special variety of Red Reishi that’s a level above traditional Red Reishi mushrooms in terms of quality and nutritional value. Through scientific and strict cultivation process with the right combination of light, temperature and humidity, Red Reishi mushroom loses its umbrella shape and form the shape of deer horn. This unique shape allowed its spores to be effectively retained. The result is deer horn shaped Red Reishi with key ingredients such as β-Glucan at around 60% which is way above industry’s norm.
