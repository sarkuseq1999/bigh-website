## Opportunity

Our goal is to bring natural and honest nutritional supplements to enhance the quality of life of our customers worldwide. In the process, we also want to create opportunities that will benefit as many people and stores as possible.

BiGH offer three types of opportunities:

* Dealership in Retail Stores (Currently available in selected countries, please contact store@bighnow.com for more dealership information).
* Members who sell BiGH products to their customers and create a network of members who do the same (Please contact member@bighnow.com for more information).
* Customers who purchase from BiGH directly.
