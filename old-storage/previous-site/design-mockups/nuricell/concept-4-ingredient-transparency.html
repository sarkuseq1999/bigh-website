The science it stands on

It started in a lab at Berkeley.

Mitochondria are the power plants inside every cell. They make the energy — ATP — that thinking, remembering, and focusing all depend on. With age, mitochondria decay. Oxidative damage builds up in their lipids, proteins, RNA and DNA. They make less energy and leak more damage.

That decline tracks closely with the memory loss and mental fatigue most adults start to feel. This is the mitochondrial theory of brain aging — and it is the idea NuriCell is built on.

The science started in a lab at Berkeley. NuriCell finishes the sentence in a capsule.

Dr. Jian Kang Liu
Biochemist · with Dr. Bruce Ames, Division of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology, UC Berkeley
2002
The landmark finding

Feeding two mitochondrial nutrients — acetyl-L-carnitine and R-alpha-lipoic acid — to old rats partially reversed age-associated brain mitochondrial decay and improved their performance on memory tasks. The combination worked better than either nutrient alone. Electron-microscope imaging showed aged mitochondria structurally restored toward a younger state.

The Carrier

It moves the fuel.

N-Acetyl L-Carnitine HCl

A mitochondrial nutrient that shuttles fuel into the cell's power plants — and, unlike many nutrients, crosses the blood–brain barrier to do that work where memory lives. Without a carrier, the fuel never reaches the furnace.

Its piece of the science

One of the two nutrients in Dr. Liu's PNAS-published research. Fed to aging animals, acetyl-L-carnitine helped restore mitochondrial function and improved performance on memory tasks. Studied in humans for memory and mental energy.

Alpha-Lipoic Acid

A mitochondrial antioxidant. It neutralizes the oxidative damage that aging power plants leak — and regenerates other antioxidants, vitamins C and E and glutathione, so the cell's whole defense network keeps working.

The Messenger

Choline Bitartrate

A building block of acetylcholine — the neurotransmitter the brain uses for memory and learning — and of healthy brain-cell membranes. Energy is only useful if the cells can still send a clear message.

Choline supplies the raw material for the signaling chemistry of memory. It completes the system: the Carrier and Protector restore the power plants, the Reservoir keeps charge on hand, and the Messenger keeps the lines of memory open.
